信惭戒意财，是法雅士誉，斯道明智说，如是升天世。愚不修天行，亦不誉布施，信施助善者，从是到彼安。信者真人长，念法所住安，近者意得上，智寿寿中贤。信能得道，法致灭度，从闻得智，所到有明。信能度渊，摄为船师，精进除苦，慧到彼岸。士有信行，为圣所誉，乐无为者，一切缚解。信之与戒，慧意能行，健夫度恚，从是脱渊。信使戒诚，亦受智慧，在在能行，处处见养。比方世利，慧信为明，是财上宝，家产非常。欲见诸真，乐听讲法，能舍悭垢，此之为信。信能度河，其福难夺，能禁止盗，野沙门乐。无信不习，好剥正言，如拙取水，掘泉扬泥。贤夫习智，乐仰清流，如善取水，思令不扰。信不染他，唯贤与人，可好则学，非好则远。信为我舆，莫知斯载，如大象调，自调最胜。信财戒财，惭愧亦财，闻财施财，慧为七财。从信守戒，常净观法，慧而利行，奉敬不忘。生有此财，不问男女，终以不贫，贤者识真。

逐句解释

信惭戒意财，是法雅士誉，斯道明智说，如是升天世。

信、惭、戒、意、财，这五者是法中雅士的声誉所在，明智者如此说道，依此修行可升天界。

Faith, conscience, virtue, discernment, wealth of practice — these are what noble persons are praised for; the wise declare this path leads to heavenly rebirth.

The verse lists the inner wealth that truly matters. Unlike material wealth, these inner qualities cannot be stolen or lost through circumstance.

愚不修天行，亦不誉布施，信施助善者，从是到彼安。

愚者不修天行，也不赞叹布施；有信心而布施、帮助善者的人，由此到达彼岸的安乐。

The foolish neither cultivate heavenly virtue nor praise giving; those with faith who give and aid the good thereby reach safety on the farther shore.

The contrast is between the closed fist of the unbeliever and the open hand of faith. Generosity flows naturally from genuine faith — it is faith in action.

信者真人长，念法所住安，近者意得上，智寿寿中贤。

有信心的人真实地成长，忆念正法而安住，亲近法者心意向上，智慧之寿在寿命中最为贤良。

One of faith grows in authenticity; dwelling in mindfulness of the Dharma one is secure; the mind of one who draws near rises upward — wisdom's lifespan is the finest span.

Faith here is not passive belief but active orientation. It creates stability, draws one toward better things, and gives the life a quality that mere years cannot provide.

信能得道，法致灭度，从闻得智，所到有明。

信能得道，法能致涅槃，从闻思得智慧，所到之处皆有光明。

Faith enables one to reach the path; the Dharma brings liberation; wisdom is gained through hearing; wherever one arrives, there is clarity.

A chain of four: faith opens the path, the path leads to liberation, hearing generates wisdom, and wisdom brings light into every situation.

信能度渊，摄为船师，精进除苦，慧到彼岸。

信心能度越深渊，摄受正法如同船师，精进能除去苦难，智慧到达彼岸。

Faith crosses the abyss; restraint is the ferryman; diligence removes suffering; wisdom reaches the farther shore.

The spiritual journey as a river crossing: faith gives the confidence to attempt it, restraint guides the boat, effort propels it, and wisdom is what arrives on the other side.

士有信行，为圣所誉，乐无为者，一切缚解。

有信心而践行的人，被圣者所称誉，乐于无为者，一切束缚皆解除。

One who has faith and acts on it is praised by the noble; those who delight in the unconditioned are freed from every bond.

Faith must generate action — it is not complete as mere inner assent. And the final freedom is described as delight in the unconditioned (Nibbana) — not a reluctant liberation but a joyful one.

信之与戒，慧意能行，健夫度恚，从是脱渊。

信与戒，加上能行的慧意，勇健之人度越嗔恚，从此脱离深渊。

Faith and virtue, wisdom and the capacity to practise — the vigorous person crosses over anger and by this is freed from the abyss.

Anger (恚) is specifically named as the obstacle overcome. Faith and ethics provide the stable ground; wisdom provides the understanding; vigour provides the momentum.

信使戒诚，亦受智慧，在在能行，处处见养。

信心使戒行真诚，也能够接受智慧，随处都能修行，处处都有人供养。

Faith makes virtue sincere; it also enables one to receive wisdom; wherever one is, one can practise; everywhere one finds support.

The person established in faith finds the conditions for practice available wherever they are. Genuine faith creates a self-sufficient inner environment.

比方世利，慧信为明，是财上宝，家产非常。

与世间财利相比，智慧与信心才是真正的光明，这是最高的财富宝藏，是不同于世间财产的财富。

Compared with worldly gain, wisdom and faith are true brightness — the highest treasure, a wealth unlike household possessions.

The reframing of wealth: what people typically call valuable is exposed as secondary to the inner wealth of wisdom and faith, which cannot be stolen or squandered.

欲见诸真，乐听讲法，能舍悭垢，此之为信。

想要见到诸真实，乐于听闻讲法，能够舍弃悭吝垢染，这就是真正的信心。

Wanting to see all that is true; delighting in hearing the teaching; able to abandon the stain of miserliness — this is faith.

Faith is described behaviorally: desire to know the truth, openness to teaching, and the generosity that flows from it. These are its recognizable marks.

信能度河，其福难夺，能禁止盗，野沙门乐。

信心能度越生死之河，其福报难以夺取，能禁止偷盗，旷野中的沙门因此而安乐。

Faith crosses the river; its merit cannot be taken away; it restrains theft; the forest monk delights in it.

Faith as asset: its merit is inalienable — unlike material goods, no one can steal it. The forest monk's delight indicates that faith is its own reward, independent of external circumstances.

无信不习，好剥正言，如拙取水，掘泉扬泥。

无信不修习，喜欢曲解正法之言，就像笨拙地取水，挖泉而扬起泥土。

Without faith one does not train; one distorts right speech; like a clumsy person drawing water, digging a spring but stirring up only mud.

Without faith, even exposure to the teaching produces distortion rather than clarity — like digging for clean water but getting mud. Right relationship to the teaching requires the receptivity that faith provides.

贤夫习智，乐仰清流，如善取水，思令不扰。

贤者修习智慧，乐于仰望清净之流，如同善于取水的人，思虑令水不扰动。

The wise cultivate wisdom and delight in looking up toward the clear stream; like one who draws water skillfully, they think in ways that do not disturb.

Skillful drawing of water: still the mind before engaging, and clarity rises naturally. The practitioner's quality of thought — whether it agitates or clarifies — determines what they receive from the teaching.

信不染他，唯贤与人，可好则学，非好则远。

真正的信心不被他人染污，唯有贤者才能给予他人，可以学习的就学，不可学习的就远离。

True faith is not contaminated by others; only the wise can give it to others; what is good, study; what is not good, avoid.

Genuine faith is individually verified — not borrowed from social pressure or authority. It comes from wisdom, and the wise can recognise and pass it on.

信为我舆，莫知斯载，如大象调，自调最胜。

信心是我的车乘，没有人知道它所承载的，就像调伏的大象，自我调伏是最殊胜的。

Faith is my vehicle; none knows what it carries; like a trained elephant, self-taming is supreme.

Faith carries us without announcing what it bears — the inner transformation is invisible to others, known only through its fruits. Self-discipline, like the trained elephant, is the greatest power.

信财戒财，惭愧亦财，闻财施财，慧为七财。

信心是财富，戒律是财富，惭与愧也是财富，多闻是财富，布施是财富，智慧是第七种财富。

Faith is wealth; virtue is wealth; conscience is wealth; hearing is wealth; giving is wealth; wisdom is the seventh wealth.

The seven noble treasures (ariya-dhana) — a complete inner economy that supports liberation. Unlike the seven forms of outer wealth, these compound rather than compete.

从信守戒，常净观法，慧而利行，奉敬不忘。

从信心出发，护持戒律，常以清净心观察正法，以智慧而有益地行动，奉敬修行从不忘失。

Beginning from faith, keeping the precepts, constantly and purely contemplating the Dharma, acting beneficially through wisdom, and not forgetting devotion —

The practice sequence: faith initiates, virtue stabilizes, contemplation deepens, wisdom directs action, and sustained devotion maintains the whole. This is the complete path in miniature.

生有此财，不问男女，终以不贫，贤者识真。

生命中拥有这些财富，不论男女，终究不会贫穷，贤者由此认识真理。

One born with these treasures — whether man or woman — will never ultimately be poor; the wise recognize this as truth.

The seven inner treasures are equally accessible regardless of gender or social status — a notably inclusive teaching. True wealth is not a matter of birth, body, or circumstance.

总结 · Summary

笃信品十八颂，深入阐释信心的本质与价值。信心不是盲目崇拜，而是与戒律、智慧、闻思并行的内在力量，是渡越苦海的船师，是最高的财富。七种圣财——信、戒、惭、愧、闻、施、慧——构成不可夺取的内在富足，任何人只要具足这些财富，无论出身贫富、无论男女，终究不会贫乏。

The chapter on Faith presents eighteen verses on the nature and value of genuine trust in the Dharma. Faith here is not blind submission but an active inner quality that works in concert with virtue, wisdom, and hearing. The chapter introduces the seven noble treasures (faith, virtue, conscience, hearing, generosity, and wisdom) — an inner wealth available to all regardless of birth or gender, and one that cannot be taken away.
