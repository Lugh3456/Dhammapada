第二十二章 · 述佛品

法句经 · Dhammapada

← 第二十一章 目录 Contents 第二十三章 →

已胜不复回，生于世间者，佛迹无能寻，何处乃能见？虽有欲网缚，超之以无累，此人能令断，如截流中草。一念以净意，化身如白净，此则为梵天，意净则宫殿。是能自舍者，我说为婆罗门。无欲无有畏，恬淡无忧患，欲除使结解，是谓为长老。见苦知其集，渡之以道品，能知老死尽，是谓最正觉。先当喻彼已，然后喻他人，善御彼自者，不以烦恼失。当知彼善御，端行无有失，无上之正觉，恒以自法益。既度彼苦际，不复生为人，所谓至道者，自致无上觉。以觉悟彼觉，本无而自通，已超越有无，以道制群生。佛不见苦集，亦非无明者，以独觉见觉，非有亦非无。一切诸世间，莫能知其真，唯有佛智慧，乃能分别之。人以爱欲生，觉意以道除，善解众苦者，苦可得灭尽。度诸忧悲情，恒无怖与惧，唯有佛法海，无量无边岸。

逐句解释

已胜不复回，生于世间者，佛迹无能寻，何处乃能见？

已经超越而不再回头，生于世间者，佛的踪迹无法寻找，在哪里才能见到？

Having conquered and not returning; born into the world — the Buddha's footprints cannot be traced; where can one see him?

The Buddha has transcended the conditioned world entirely. His footprints (no karma, no rebirth) cannot be tracked. He cannot be found in ordinary places.

虽有欲网缚，超之以无累，此人能令断，如截流中草。

虽然有欲望的网络束缚，以无执累超越之，此人能令（欲望）断除，如同截断水流中的草。

Though bound by the net of desires, transcending through freedom from accumulation; such a person can sever (desires) — like cutting grass in a stream.

Desire-net and its transcendence: the image of cutting grass in the current — not fighting the water (desire) but severing the attachment.

一念以净意，化身如白净，此则为梵天，意净则宫殿。

以清净意念，化身如白净（光明），此则为梵天，意净则（自然处于）宫殿之中。

With a single moment of pure intention, the body transforms like white purity; this is Brahma-heaven; with pure intention one is in the palace.

Purity of intention transforms one's reality instantly. Heaven is not a place to travel to but a state generated by pure mind.

是能自舍者，我说为婆罗门。

能够自我舍弃（执著）的，我称之为婆罗门。

One who can relinquish the self — this, I say, is a brahmin.

True brahminhood is not birth but the capacity to let go. The Buddha's redefinition: the title belongs to those who have relinquished self-clinging.

无欲无有畏，恬淡无忧患，欲除使结解，是谓为长老。

无欲则无所畏惧，恬淡而无忧患，欲望除尽，使缚结解脱，这叫做长老（真正的尊者）。

Without desire there is no fear; serene and without worry; desires exhausted, the bonds of defilements resolved — this is called an elder.

Genuine seniority (长老) redefined: not age but the completion of the path — desires exhausted, bonds dissolved, fearlessness achieved.

见苦知其集，渡之以道品，能知老死尽，是谓最正觉。

见到苦，知道苦的集起，以道品度脱之，能知老死的终尽，这叫做最正觉。

Seeing suffering and knowing its arising; crossing through with the path-factors; knowing the exhaustion of aging and death — this is called supreme enlightenment.

The Four Truths condensed: seeing suffering, knowing its origin, crossing via the path, knowing the end of aging and death = perfect enlightenment.

先当喻彼已，然后喻他人，善御彼自者，不以烦恼失。

先当开示自己，然后再开示他人，善于驾御自己者，不因烦恼而失道。

First illuminate oneself, then illuminate others; one who governs oneself well does not lose the path through defilements.

Self-illumination precedes other-illumination. Self-governance is the protection against being derailed by defilements.

当知彼善御，端行无有失，无上之正觉，恒以自法益。

应当知道那善于驾御（自己）者，行为端正无有过失，具有无上正觉，恒以自身之法利益（众生）。

Know that one who governs well has conduct that is upright and without fault; possessing supreme enlightenment, one constantly benefits beings through the Dharma.

Upright conduct + supreme enlightenment → constant benefit to others through the Dharma. This is the Buddha's own example.

既度彼苦际，不复生为人，所谓至道者，自致无上觉。

既已度脱苦的彼岸，不再生为人，所谓到达道者，自致证得无上觉。

Having crossed beyond the shore of suffering; no longer born as a person; the one who is said to have reached the path — self-accomplished, attaining supreme enlightenment.

The fully accomplished one: having crossed the ocean of suffering, no longer subject to rebirth, having attained supreme enlightenment through their own effort.

以觉悟彼觉，本无而自通，已超越有无，以道制群生。

以觉悟来觉悟彼觉，本来无而自通，已超越有与无，以道调御群生。

Through awakening, awakening to that awakening; originally nothing but self-penetrating; having transcended both being and non-being — guiding beings through the Dharma.

The non-dual awakening: transcending both existence and non-existence, yet fully present to guide beings. This is the Buddha's paradoxical position.

佛不见苦集，亦非无明者，以独觉见觉，非有亦非无。

佛不见到苦集（为实有），也不是无明者，以独觉见觉，非有亦非无。

The Buddha does not see the arising of suffering as truly existing; nor is he one of ignorance; through self-awakening he sees awakening — neither existing nor not existing.

The Buddha's position is beyond all views: not grasping existence, not falling into nihilism, self-awakened and beyond the categories of existing and not-existing.

一切诸世间，莫能知其真，唯有佛智慧，乃能分别之。

一切诸世间，无法知道其真实，唯有佛的智慧，才能分别（了解）它。

None throughout all the worlds can know its reality; only the Buddha's wisdom can distinguish and understand it.

The unique scope of Buddha-wisdom: it alone comprehends the true nature of reality. This is not a claim to be believed but an invitation to attain the same wisdom.

人以爱欲生，觉意以道除，善解众苦者，苦可得灭尽。

人因爱欲而生，觉意以道除之，善于解除众苦者，苦可得彻底灭尽。

Beings are born through love and desire; the awakening-mind removes this through the path; one who skillfully resolves all suffering — suffering can be completely extinguished.

From birth (through craving) to complete extinction (through the path): the entire arc of the Buddhist project in one verse.

度诸忧悲情，恒无怖与惧，唯有佛法海，无量无边岸。

度脱诸忧悲情感，恒常无有恐怖与惧怕，唯有佛法之海，无量无边岸。

Crossing beyond all grief and lamentation; constantly without terror or fear; only the ocean of the Buddha-Dharma — without measure, without a far shore.

The Dharma as an ocean: boundless in scope, inexhaustible in depth. This is both its challenge and its beauty.

总结 · Summary

述佛品十四颂，以无处可寻开篇，以无量无边结尾。佛迹不可寻，净意即梵天，能自舍者为婆罗门，无欲无畏者为长老，见苦知集度以道品为正觉。先照亮自己再照亮他人。佛以独觉见觉，非有非无；唯佛智慧知世间真实；唯佛法海无量无边。

The Buddha presents fourteen verses moving from 'his footprints cannot be traced' to 'the ocean of the Dharma is boundless.' Pure intention is Brahma-heaven; self-renunciation is true brahminhood; desirelessness is true eldership; seeing suffering and its origin is supreme enlightenment. The Buddha transcends all categories of being and non-being; his wisdom alone knows reality fully; the Dharma-ocean has no far shore — it is inexhaustible.
