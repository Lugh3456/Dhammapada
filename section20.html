第二十章 · 爱身品

法句经 · Dhammapada

← 第十九章 目录 Contents 第二十一章 →

自爱身者，慎护所守，希望欲解，学正不寐。为身第一，常自勉修，利后世者，必先自治。身为第一，当立以圣，端心正意，是应最乐。若己不如是，焉能喻彼？身如本自治，然后喻他人。己心正化，然后化人，谨慎以行，法本自然。身之过患，心而自觉，不以恶行，不以善行。夫自念身者，日夜莫违教，解脱有苦乐，自致得涅槃。夫人相爱，莫能勤道，便同群生，老死自然。自利利人，自利者多。为善不得善，为恶得恶，心念清净者，必得受其福。常以慈心，护彼众生，念彼苦者，必入安乐。自爱则自治，已治则道立，道立则福乐。

逐句解释

自爱身者，慎护所守，希望欲解，学正不寐。

爱护自己的人，谨慎守护所持守的（戒律），希望得到解脱，学习正法而不懈怠。

One who loves oneself guards carefully what is to be guarded; hoping for liberation, one studies the Dharma without sleeping.

True self-love is defined at the outset: it is not indulgence but careful guarding of one's conduct, oriented toward liberation.

为身第一，常自勉修，利后世者，必先自治。

以（修行）为第一要务，常常自我勉励修习，利益来世的人，必定先从自我治理开始。

Making practice the first priority; always exhorting oneself in cultivation; one who benefits future lives must first govern oneself.

Self-governance as the foundation: future benefit (in this and subsequent lives) requires present self-discipline.

身为第一，当立以圣，端心正意，是应最乐。

以身（修行）为第一，应当立身于圣道，端正心意，这是最大的快乐。

Placing the body-in-practice as first; one should establish oneself on the noble path; upright in mind and thought — this is the supreme happiness.

The supreme happiness is not pleasure but the alignment of one's life with the noble path.

若己不如是，焉能喻彼？身如本自治，然后喻他人。

若自己不能如此，怎能开示他人？自身如同从根本上治理，然后才能开示他人。

If one is not like this oneself, how can one guide others? When the body is governed from the root, then one can guide others.

The principle of modeling: genuine guidance of others is only possible from the foundation of one's own practice.

己心正化，然后化人，谨慎以行，法本自然。

先正化自己的心，然后再化导他人，行事谨慎，法本来就是如此自然。

First rectify and transform one's own mind, then transform others; act with care — the Dharma is naturally so.

The sequence is non-negotiable: self-transformation precedes other-transformation. This is simply the nature of things.

身之过患，心而自觉，不以恶行，不以善行。

身体的过患，心中自我觉察，不因此做出恶行，也不因此做出善行（执著于）。

The faults of the body — the mind becomes self-aware; not performing evil actions thereby; not performing good actions with attachment.

A subtler teaching: mindfulness of the body's problems brings awareness, and that awareness guides conduct without either vice or its opposite becoming a new form of attachment.

夫自念身者，日夜莫违教，解脱有苦乐，自致得涅槃。

那些自己思念（修行）身体（实践）的人，日夜不违背教诫，解脱有苦有乐，自然能够证得涅槃。

One who keeps the body's practice constantly in mind, day and night not violating the teaching — liberation has both suffering and happiness; one naturally attains Nirvana.

Consistency is the key: day and night, without violation. The path has both difficult and joyful elements, but the destination is certain.

夫人相爱，莫能勤道，便同群生，老死自然。

人们互相爱恋，却不能勤于修道，便与众生相同，自然走向老死。

People love one another but cannot be diligent in the path; they become the same as ordinary beings — naturally aging and dying.

Attachment even to those we love can become a distraction from practice. The result: the ordinary cycle of aging and dying, unredeemed.

自利利人，自利者多。

自利（修道）同时也利于他人，自利（者）确实更多。

Benefiting oneself benefits others; truly, self-benefit is the greater.

Counter-intuitive but profound: genuine self-cultivation — developing wisdom and virtue — produces more benefit for others than direct but ungrounded attempts to help.

为善不得善，为恶得恶，心念清净者，必得受其福。

做善而不执著得善，做恶则得恶报，心念清净的人，必然接受其福报。

Doing good without expecting to receive good; doing evil, one receives evil; one whose mind-intention is pure — necessarily receives blessings.

Purity of intention rather than calculation of results: genuine goodness is not done for reward, yet genuine goodness does bear fruit.

常以慈心，护彼众生，念彼苦者，必入安乐。

常以慈心守护那众生，念及彼等苦者，必然进入安乐。

Always with loving-kindness, protecting all beings; contemplating their suffering — one necessarily enters peace and happiness.

Empathy as a path to one's own peace: contemplating others' suffering motivates compassionate action, and that action produces inner peace.

自爱则自治，已治则道立，道立则福乐。

自爱则自我治理，自我治理则道得以建立，道立则福乐现前。

Self-love leads to self-governance; self-governance establishes the path; the path established, blessings and joy appear.

The logical chain: self-love → self-governance → path → blessings. This is the entire chapter in four steps.

总结 · Summary

爱身品十二颂，重新定义了自爱：真正的自爱是谨慎守护戒律，以修行为第一要务，先治理自己再化导他人。相爱而不修道，仍随众生老死。自利利人，心念清净者必得其福。自爱→自治→道立→福乐，这就是爱身品的四步逻辑。

The Self presents twelve verses that redefine self-love. Genuine self-love means guarding one's precepts, making practice the first priority, governing oneself before guiding others. Love between people without practice still ends in the ordinary cycle of aging and death. Benefiting oneself genuinely benefits others. The chapter's logic: self-love → self-governance → the path established → blessings.
