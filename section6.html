法句经 · Dhammapada

出息入息念，具满谛思惟，从初竟通利，安如佛所说。是则照世间，如云解月现，起止学思惟，坐卧不废忘。比丘立是念，前利后则胜，始得终必胜，逝不睹生死。若见身所住，六更以为最，比丘常一心，便自知泥洹。已有是诸念，自身常建行，若其不如是，终不得意行。是随本行者，如是度爱劳，若能悟意念，知解一心乐。应时等行法，是度老死恼，比丘悟意行，当令应是念。诸念生死弃，为能作苦际，常当听微妙，自觉悟其意。能觉者为贤，终始无所会，以觉意能应，日夜务学行。当解甘露要，令诸漏得尽，夫人得善利，乃来自归佛。己知自觉意，是为佛弟子，常当昼夜念，佛与法及僧。念身念非常，念戒布施德，空不愿无相，昼夜当念是。

逐句解释

出息入息念，具满谛思惟，从初竟通利，安如佛所说。

专注出息入息，完整而真实地思惟，从始至终通达清晰，安住于佛所说的修行方法。

Mindfulness of breath — in-breathing and out-breathing — fully, with careful attention; from start to finish clear and smooth, as the Buddha taught.

Anapanasati (mindfulness of breathing) is introduced as the primary method of the chapter. The Buddha's instruction is to apply awareness with completeness — not partial attention but full presence.

是则照世间，如云解月现，起止学思惟，坐卧不废忘。

此法照耀世间，如同云散月出，无论行住坐卧都在修学思惟，片刻不废忘。

This illuminates the world like the moon appearing when clouds disperse; whether rising, standing, sitting or lying, the practice of mindfulness is never abandoned.

The simile of the moon emerging from clouds: the mind's natural luminosity has always been there — mindfulness practice removes the obscuration.

比丘立是念，前利后则胜，始得终必胜，逝不睹生死。

比丘确立这种正念，前面的修行有益，后面则更胜，从一开始就能最终殊胜，逝者不再见到生死。

A monk who establishes this mindfulness benefits now and gains still more later; from the beginning one reaches final victory; the departed no longer behold birth and death.

Mindfulness is progressive in its effects — each stage of practice deepens the next, culminating in liberation from the cycle of rebirth.

若见身所住，六更以为最，比丘常一心，便自知泥洹。

若能见到身体所住之处，以六入处的观察为最重要，比丘常保一心，便能自知涅槃。

If one sees the place where the body dwells and takes the six bases as most important — a monk who constantly maintains one-pointedness of mind thereby knows Nirvana directly.

The six sense bases (eye, ear, nose, tongue, body, mind) as objects of mindful observation — watching them without grasping leads directly to the cessation of craving.

已有是诸念，自身常建行，若其不如是，终不得意行。

已经具足这些正念，自身常常建立修行，若非如此，终究不能如意修行。

Having established these mindfulness practices and continually building this practice in oneself — if one does not do this, one will never accomplish the practice as intended.

Intention without practice does not produce the result. The mindfulness must be established and maintained — not merely understood intellectually.

是随本行者，如是度爱劳，若能悟意念，知解一心乐。

随本行而修行者，如此度越爱欲的劳苦，若能觉悟意念，懂得理解一心之乐。

One who follows the fundamental practice thereby crosses the toil of craving; one who can awaken the mind of mindfulness knows and understands the joy of one-pointedness.

Love's labor — craving — is crossed not by fighting it but by the sustained attention that sees through it. The joy of unified mind is its own reward.

应时等行法，是度老死恼，比丘悟意行，当令应是念。

在适当时机平等地修行正法，这是度越老死之苦的方法，比丘觉悟意行，应当令心符合这种正念。

Practising the Dharma with equanimity at the right time — this crosses the affliction of aging and death; a monk who awakens the mind's practice should keep the mind in accord with this mindfulness.

Timing and equanimity together: practice at the right moment with a balanced mind is what dissolves the most fundamental suffering — aging and death.

诸念生死弃，为能作苦际，常当听微妙，自觉悟其意。

诸种念头于生死中舍弃，这是能够作为苦之边际的修行，常常听闻微妙法义，自觉悟其意。

Abandoning all thoughts in birth and death — this practice reaches the end of suffering; always hear the subtle teaching and awaken the mind of understanding for oneself.

The 'end of suffering' (苦际) is the horizon toward which all mindfulness practice tends. Self-awakening — not borrowed from another — is the final step.

能觉者为贤，终始无所会，以觉意能应，日夜务学行。

能够觉悟的人才是贤者，终始没有（执著的）所会，以觉悟的心应对一切，日夜务必修学行道。

One who can awaken is truly wise; from beginning to end there is nothing to cling to; with an awakened mind one responds appropriately; day and night one strives in study and practice.

The awakened mind is not a state achieved once and held — it responds freshly to each situation, and the practice that sustains it continues day and night without interruption.

当解甘露要，令诸漏得尽，夫人得善利，乃来自归佛。

应当解明甘露的要义，使种种烦恼漏尽，人若得到了真正的善利，便会来归依佛陀。

Understand the essence of the nectar-like teaching; let all taints be exhausted; when a person gains genuine benefit, they naturally come to take refuge in the Buddha.

The nectar is the teaching itself — life-giving, sweet, and healing. When it genuinely reaches someone, taking refuge in the Three Jewels is the natural response.

己知自觉意，是为佛弟子，常当昼夜念，佛与法及僧。

自知自觉意，这才是佛弟子，应当昼夜常念，佛与法及僧。

One who knows and awakens their own mind — this is a true disciple of the Buddha; day and night one should constantly be mindful of the Buddha, Dharma, and Sangha.

Self-awakening and refuge in the Three Jewels are not opposites — the disciple who understands their own mind turns naturally toward the Buddha, Dharma, and Sangha as the path that made this understanding possible.

念身念非常，念戒布施德，空不愿无相，昼夜当念是。

念身体之无常，念戒律与布施之德，念空、无愿、无相三解脱门，昼夜应当如此念。

Be mindful of the body's impermanence; be mindful of virtue and the merit of giving; the three gates of liberation — emptiness, signlessness, and wishlessness — these should be contemplated day and night.

The six objects of continual mindfulness named here together constitute a complete map of Buddhist practice: impermanence of the body, virtue, generosity, and the three entrances to liberation.

总结 · Summary

惟念品十二颂，以入出息念（数息观）为核心，阐述正念修行的方法与功德。正念如月光照耀世间，在行住坐卧中不废忘，从始至终地修行，能度越爱欲与老死之苦，最终自知涅槃。品末归纳六种正念的对象：身体无常、戒律功德、布施之德，以及空、无愿、无相三解脱门，构成正念修行的完整地图。

The chapter on Mindfulness presents twelve verses centred on mindfulness of breathing (anapanasati) as the primary practice, describing how sustained, continuous mindfulness illuminates the mind like moonlight breaking through clouds. The chapter traces the full arc from establishing the practice through to the direct knowledge of Nirvana, and concludes with six objects of continual mindfulness — forming a complete map of the contemplative path.
