第三十章 · 地狱品

法句经 · Dhammapada

← 第二十九章 目录 Contents 第三十一章 →

妄语入地狱，作之言不作，二罪后俱受，自欺者亦然。袈裟被其身，为恶不自禁，恶者入恶道，地狱中自牵。无戒不摄心，出家为邪行，贪欲以自坏，是谓非法行。为无戒律行，妄称沙门名，受食如燃铁，虚假之道人。放逸有四事，犯戒与邪行，不善法以恢，是谓堕地狱。犯戒虽天赐，虽近亦远之，以善行所招，岂非福报耶？恶人畏善人，如牛畏豹虎，善者见恶者，亦惧彼染污。人为众苦逼，莫知灭苦道，宁令此众生，见彼苦际乐。

逐句解释

妄语入地狱，作之言不作，二罪后俱受，自欺者亦然。

说妄语者入地狱，做了（坏事）说没做，两种罪后来都要受，自欺者也是如此。

False speech leads to hell; doing something and saying one did not — both sins must be received afterward; one who deceives oneself is the same.

False speech and self-deception are the twin roots of hell-bound conduct. The self-deceiver who lies to themselves faces the same consequences as those who lie to others.

袈裟被其身，为恶不自禁，恶者入恶道，地狱中自牵。

袈裟披于其身，（若）为恶而不自禁，恶者入恶道，地狱中自我牵引。

With the robe draped on the body; if one does evil without restraint — the evil one enters the evil path; in hell, pulling oneself in.

The robed person who acts evilly goes to hell through their own conduct — 'pulling oneself in.' The robe is no protection against unrestrained evil.

无戒不摄心，出家为邪行，贪欲以自坏，是谓非法行。

无戒不摄心，出家却是邪行，贪欲以自坏，这称为非法行。

Without precepts and not restraining the mind; having left home, practicing wrongly; with craving destroying oneself — this is called non-Dharmic conduct.

The monk without precepts or mind-restraint is practicing wrongly. Craving is self-destructive. The robe without practice is meaningless.

为无戒律行，妄称沙门名，受食如燃铁，虚假之道人。

（若）无戒律行，妄称沙门之名，接受（供养）食物如同吞燃烧的铁，虚假之道人。

Practicing without precepts; falsely claiming the name of monk; receiving food-offerings like swallowing burning iron; a false person of the path.

One of the most severe images in the T0210: the false monk who accepts offerings without genuine practice swallows molten iron. The consequence is proportional to the hypocrisy.

放逸有四事，犯戒与邪行，不善法以恢，是谓堕地狱。

放逸有四事，犯戒与邪行，不善法扩张，这称为堕地狱。

Heedlessness has four things: violating precepts and wrong conduct; unwholesome dharmas expanding — this is called falling into hell.

The four-part path to hell: heedlessness, precept-violation, wrong conduct, expansion of the unwholesome. Each enables the next.

犯戒虽天赐，虽近亦远之，以善行所招，岂非福报耶？

犯戒者虽获天赐，虽（与善人）靠近也如远离，以善行所招，岂非福报耶？

The precept-violator, though divinely endowed; though near (to the good) is as if far — what is attracted by good conduct, is that not blessed fruit?

Proximity to the good without practice is no help. Only good conduct attracts blessed fruit. The precept-violator remains remote from blessing regardless of circumstance.

恶人畏善人，如牛畏豹虎，善者见恶者，亦惧彼染污。

恶人畏惧善人，如牛畏豹虎，善者见恶者，也惧怕被彼染污。

The evil person fears the good as cattle fear leopards and tigers; the good person seeing the evil person also fears being contaminated by them.

Mutual fear as a measure of moral distance: the evil person fears exposure; the good person fears contamination. The gap between them is significant.

人为众苦逼，莫知灭苦道，宁令此众生，见彼苦际乐。

人被众多苦所逼迫，不知道灭苦之道，宁愿令此众生，见到彼苦际的安乐。

People are oppressed by many sufferings; not knowing the path to end suffering — better to lead these beings to see the joy at the shore of suffering's end.

Compassion as the response to hell: seeing beings trapped in suffering without knowing the way out, the practitioner's motivation is to show them liberation.

地狱品终。

地狱品终。妄语入地狱，袈裟不救恶，假僧吞铁，放逸四事堕落。知苦之路，导众生见苦际之乐。

End of the chapter on Hell. False speech leads to hell; the robe does not protect the evil; the false monk swallows iron; heedlessness's four things lead to falling. Knowing the path out of suffering — lead beings to the joy at suffering's end.

Closing summary.

总结 · Summary

地狱品八颂，以妄语入地狱开篇，以导众生见苦际之乐结尾。袈裟不救恶，假僧吞铁（最严厉之象），放逸四事是堕落之路。善者惧被恶染，恶者惧善如牛惧豹虎。章末转向慈悲：见众生受苦不知出路，引导他们见苦际之乐。

Hell presents eight verses moving from false speech as the gate of hell to compassion as the response to seeing beings trapped in suffering. The robe is no protection against unrestrained evil. The false monk who accepts offerings without practice swallows burning iron — one of the T0210's most severe images. The four-part path to hell: heedlessness → precept-violation → wrong conduct → expansion of the unwholesome. The chapter closes with compassion: seeing beings trapped in suffering, the practitioner's impulse is to show them liberation.
