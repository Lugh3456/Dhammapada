第三十四章 · 沙门品

法句经 · Dhammapada

← 第三十三章 目录 Contents 第三十五章 →

沙门何行？如何住戒律？能堪入涅槃？名誉过于人。端身守口意，以三业清净，戒行皆具足，是谓真沙门。若沙门以威仪，护身守其心，内有清净行，是为沙门行。不以手犯人，亦不以口犯，意亦不犯者，是谓身口意。贤者妙行，多所修习，护身守口，心无所错。慎言守意，不犯诸戒，行三法者，佛道无为。比丘当观身，若见外异相，内所以如何，令彼无所有。执持以净行，奉法而不欺，若沙门如是，悉当得道果。上下诸方界，诸众生皆苦，沙门于此道，离世间安住。诸比丘善观，观内以清净，以坐自觉悟，是谓真沙门。

逐句解释

沙门何行？如何住戒律？能堪入涅槃？名誉过于人。

沙门行什么？如何住于戒律？能堪入涅槃？名誉超过他人。

What does a monk practice? How does one dwell in precepts? Who can enter Nirvana? Whose renown surpasses others?

The chapter opens with four questions that its verses then answer. The framing device invites the reader to hold these questions as they read.

端身守口意，以三业清净，戒行皆具足，是谓真沙门。

端正身、守护口、调御意，以三业清净，戒行皆具足，这称为真沙门。

Upright in body, guarding speech, governing mind — purifying the three karmas; with virtuous conduct fully accomplished — this is called a true monk.

The definition of the true monk: three-karma purity (body, speech, mind) and complete virtuous conduct.

若沙门以威仪，护身守其心，内有清净行，是为沙门行。

若沙门以威仪，守护身守其心，内有清净行，这是沙门行。

If a monk with dignified bearing guards the body and protects the mind; with pure practice within — this is the monk's conduct.

Dignified bearing is not mere appearance but the outward expression of inner purity. The three together (bearing, guarding, inner purity) constitute genuine monastic conduct.

不以手犯人，亦不以口犯，意亦不犯者，是谓身口意。

不以手侵犯他人，也不以口侵犯，意念也不侵犯者，这称为身口意（清净）。

Not attacking others with the hands; not attacking with the mouth; not attacking with mind-intention — this is called body, speech, and mind (purity).

Three-karma non-aggression: no physical harm, no verbal harm, no mental harm. The complete definition of non-violence at all levels.

贤者妙行，多所修习，护身守口，心无所错。

贤者妙行，多加修习，守护身、守护口，心无所错失。

The wise person's excellent conduct — much cultivated; guarding body, guarding speech — mind without error.

Much cultivation as the path to excellent conduct. The three guards (body, speech, mind) with the mind free of error.

慎言守意，不犯诸戒，行三法者，佛道无为。

慎言守护意念，不违犯诸戒，行三种法者，佛道无为。

Careful speech and guarding the mind; not violating the precepts; one who practices these three dharmas — the Buddha-path of non-action.

The three dharmas of Buddhist practice: careful speech, mind-guarding, non-violation of precepts. Together they constitute the non-active (effortless) path.

比丘当观身，若见外异相，内所以如何，令彼无所有。

比丘应当观察身，若见到外在异相，内在所以如何，令彼（心）无所有。

A monk should observe the body; if seeing external unusual appearances, what is happening within? — causing that (mind) to have nothing.

Body-observation as the corrective to external appearances. When unusual signs attract attention outward, the practice is to observe the internal cause and bring the mind to emptiness.

执持以净行，奉法而不欺，若沙门如是，悉当得道果。

执持清净行，奉持法而不欺，若沙门如此，皆当得道果。

Holding firmly to pure conduct; upholding the Dharma without deception; if a monk is like this — all will attain the fruit of the path.

Certainty of attainment: pure conduct + Dharma-upholding without deception = assured path-fruit for every such monk.

上下诸方界，诸众生皆苦，沙门于此道，离世间安住。

上下诸方界，诸众生皆苦，沙门于此道，离世间安住。

Above and below, throughout all directions — all beings suffer; the monk on this path, departing from the world, dwells in peace.

The monk's position: surrounded by universal suffering in all directions, the monk on the path dwells in peace through departure (not from the world physically but from attachment to it).

诸比丘善观，观内以清净，以坐自觉悟，是谓真沙门。

诸比丘善观，观内以清净，以坐而自觉悟，这称为真沙门。

Monks, observe well — observe within and be pure; through sitting, self-awakening — this is called the true monk.

The true monk defined ultimately by meditation: sitting, self-awakening, inner purity. All the outer marks lead here.

沙门品终。

沙门品终。三业清净为真沙门，不犯三行者得佛道无为，以坐自觉悟——内外俱净，是谓真沙门。

End of the chapter on the Monk. Three-karma purity is the true monk; not violating the three conducts attains the non-active Buddha-path; through sitting, self-awakening — inner and outer both pure; this is called the true monk.

Closing summary.

总结 · Summary

沙门品十颂，以四问开篇：沙门何行？如何住戒？能入涅槃？名誉过人？答曰：三业清净、戒行具足为真沙门；不以手口意犯人为三业不犯；慎言守意不犯戒行三法者得佛道无为；以坐自觉悟——内外俱净，是谓真沙门。

The Monk presents ten verses beginning with four questions: what does a monk practice? How does one dwell in precepts? Who can enter Nirvana? Whose renown surpasses others? The answers: three-karma purity and complete virtuous conduct; not attacking others with hand, mouth, or mind; careful speech, mind-guarding, non-violation of precepts — these three constitute the non-active Buddha-path; through sitting and self-awakening, inner and outer both pure — the true monk.
