第三十九章 · 吉祥品

法句经 · Dhammapada

← 第三十八章 目录 Contents

众士群集会，天地诸神人，皆来问善事，何为最吉祥。不亲近愚人，亦不与恶伴，亲近有德者，是为最吉祥。住处于国土，宿世善业造，自正其志向，是为最吉祥。广学多识艺，于戒律可问，所言皆善哉，是为最吉祥。奉养父母亲，爱护妻与子，从事善业者，是为最吉祥。布施持净戒，亲属善知识，无过失之行，是为最吉祥。制心离诸恶，禁酒不放逸，于法无懈怠，是为最吉祥。恭敬并谦逊，知足常感恩，及时闻正法，是为最吉祥。忍辱与柔和，常与沙门会，及时论正法，是为最吉祥。自行及教人，行法修学戒，自修以教人，是为最吉祥。以定自娱乐，独处自修道，心常有圣行，是为最吉祥。触娆而不动，忧患不能入，以道自勉励，是为最吉祥。行如是法者，一切处安隐，是人之所行，处处皆吉祥。

逐句解释

众士群集会，天地诸神人，皆来问善事，何为最吉祥。

众士群集，天地诸神人，皆来问善事，何为最吉祥。

The assembly of many gathered — gods and humans of heaven and earth — all come to ask about the good: what is the most auspicious blessing?

The final chapter opens as a cosmic assembly. Gods and humans alike come with the same question: what constitutes the highest blessing? The chapter is the answer.

不亲近愚人，亦不与恶伴，亲近有德者，是为最吉祥。

不亲近愚人，也不与恶者为伴，亲近有德者，这是最吉祥。

Not associating with the foolish; not companioning with the evil; associating with those who have virtue — this is the most auspicious blessing.

The first and foundational blessing: right association. Who we spend time with shapes who we become.

住处于国土，宿世善业造，自正其志向，是为最吉祥。

居住于国土，宿世善业所造，自正其志向，这是最吉祥。

Dwelling in a good land; made so by wholesome actions in past lives; and personally rectifying one's own aspirations — this is the most auspicious blessing.

Place + karma + personal aspiration: all three together constitute the blessing of good circumstances. Neither place alone nor karma alone suffices.

广学多识艺，于戒律可问，所言皆善哉，是为最吉祥。

广学多识技艺，于戒律可问（已知戒律），所言皆善哉，这是最吉祥。

Broad learning and much skill; knowing the precepts (able to answer on them); all that is said is excellent — this is the most auspicious blessing.

Learning, precept-knowledge, and excellent speech combined: the blessing of a cultivated person whose words can be trusted.

奉养父母亲，爱护妻与子，从事善业者，是为最吉祥。

奉养父母亲，爱护妻与子，从事善业者，这是最吉祥。

Supporting and nourishing one's parents; lovingly caring for spouse and children; being engaged in wholesome work — this is the most auspicious blessing.

Family responsibility and wholesome livelihood as blessings. This blessing is fully within the household life — the path is available there too.

布施持净戒，亲属善知识，无过失之行，是为最吉祥。

布施、持净戒，亲属善知识，无过失之行，这是最吉祥。

Generosity and upholding pure precepts; relatives as good spiritual friends; conduct without faults — this is the most auspicious blessing.

Generosity, precepts, good friends (even among family), and faultless conduct: the blessing of the virtuous householder.

制心离诸恶，禁酒不放逸，于法无懈怠，是为最吉祥。

制御心离诸恶，禁酒不放逸，于法无懈怠，这是最吉祥。

Governing the mind and departing from all evil; abstaining from intoxicants and not being heedless; with no laziness in the Dharma — this is the most auspicious blessing.

Mind-governance, sobriety, non-heedlessness, Dharma-diligence: the blessings of the disciplined practitioner. Each one builds on the previous.

恭敬并谦逊，知足常感恩，及时闻正法，是为最吉祥。

恭敬并谦逊，知足常感恩，及时闻正法，这是最吉祥。

Respectful and humble; content and always grateful; hearing the correct Dharma at the right time — this is the most auspicious blessing.

Respect, humility, contentment, gratitude, and timely Dharma-hearing: the blessings of the receptive student.

忍辱与柔和，常与沙门会，及时论正法，是为最吉祥。

忍辱与柔和，常与沙门（修行者）相会，及时论议正法，这是最吉祥。

Patience and gentleness; constantly meeting with monks; discussing the correct Dharma at the right time — this is the most auspicious blessing.

The sangha-blessing: patience, gentleness, constant association with practitioners, and timely Dharma-discussion. The community as blessing.

自行及教人，行法修学戒，自修以教人，是为最吉祥。

自行（修道）及教人（修道），行法修学戒，自修以教人，这是最吉祥。

Practicing oneself and teaching others; practicing the Dharma and studying the precepts; self-cultivation and teaching others — this is the most auspicious blessing.

The blessing of Dharma-propagation: not keeping the practice private but sharing it. Self-cultivation that naturally flows into teaching.

以定自娱乐，独处自修道，心常有圣行，是为最吉祥。

以禅定自我娱乐，独处自修道，心常有圣行，这是最吉祥。

Taking meditation as delight; dwelling alone and self-cultivating; the mind always engaged in noble conduct — this is the most auspicious blessing.

The advanced practitioner's blessing: meditation as delight (not discipline), solitary cultivation, and the constancy of noble conduct in the mind.

触娆而不动，忧患不能入，以道自勉励，是为最吉祥。

触娆（接触扰动）而不动，忧患不能入，以道自勉励，这是最吉祥。

When touched and disturbed, not moving; worry and suffering unable to enter; self-encouraged through the path — this is the most auspicious blessing.

Unshakeability: when disturbed, not moving; when suffering approaches, not being entered. This is the blessing of the fully established practitioner.

行如是法者，一切处安隐，是人之所行，处处皆吉祥。

行如此法者，一切处安隐，此人之所行，处处皆吉祥。

One who practices all these dharmas — everywhere peaceful and secure; wherever such a person goes — everywhere is auspicious blessing.

The culminating verse: the practitioner who has integrated all these blessings carries blessing wherever they go. Blessing is no longer a circumstance but a quality of presence.

总结 · Summary

吉祥品十三颂，以神人共问「何为最吉祥」开篇，以「处处皆吉祥」作结。十二种吉祥层层递进：亲近有德（人际），善地善业善志（因缘），学识戒律善言（素养），奉养父母（家庭），布施净戒善友（德行），制心禁酒不怠（修行），恭敬谦逊知足闻法（态度），忍辱柔和亲沙门（僧伽），自行教人（弘法），以定为乐（深定），触而不动（不动地）。行此法者，处处皆吉祥——吉祥不是外在条件，而是修行者自身的素质。

Blessings presents thirteen verses, the final chapter of the T0210, opening with a cosmic assembly asking 'what is the most auspicious blessing?' and closing with 'wherever such a person goes, everywhere is auspicious blessing.' The twelve blessings build from foundation to summit: right association, good circumstances, learning and precepts, family care, generosity and virtue, mind-governance and sobriety, respect and Dharma-hearing, patience and sangha, teaching others, meditation as delight, and finally unshakeability. The practitioner who integrates all these carries blessing wherever they go — blessing is not a circumstance but a quality of presence.
