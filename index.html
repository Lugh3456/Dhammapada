Dhammapada

法句经

大正藏 No. 0210 · 吴 维祇难等译

The Dhammapada (法句经) is one of Buddhism's most treasured anthologies of teaching verses. This site presents the classical Chinese T0210 translation — rendered by Vighna and Zhu Liuyan in 224 CE during the Kingdom of Wu — comprising 752 verses in 39 chapters. The terse four-character lines carry the full weight of the Dharma: impermanence, mind, virtue, the path, and liberation. 心为法本 — mind is the root of all dharmas.
