第二十六章 · 尘垢品

法句经 · Dhammapada

← 第二十五章 目录 Contents 第二十七章 →

生无善行者，是为无所有，坏其男女仪，是谓为弊漏。生能行善者，此为有所有，其行清净者，是谓无所漏。夫人闻多智，自以为明哲，生疑以自止，是谓痴所弊。悭为秽弊漏，不施为尘垢，胜慢为垢污，不学为大秽。不诵为尘垢，不勤为尘垢，懈怠为自污，非智慧之道。垢中之垢，莫甚于痴，学当舍此，比丘无垢。苟生无耻者，鸱枭之与鸟，如是厚颜人，杂居反污清。有耻者易教，无耻者难教，有耻能求善，无耻不能求。有惭则成法，无惭无所生，能自惭耻者，是谓善法中。起坐不安者，其心常驰驱，诸根悉调顺，为不清净行。身口意俱净，能护三行者，知此世彼世，是谓有净者。观身为净者，以为不净观，如是知正行，到苦际为快。诸根常调顺，如是为梵行，行行若平地，自此彼亦平。不慢俱等心，慕德贵法者，自无尘垢染，是谓无所有。学取正智慧，意以好法乐，除去众不善，是名为清净。

逐句解释

生无善行者，是为无所有，坏其男女仪，是谓为弊漏。

生而没有善行者，这是无所有，破坏了男女的仪规，这称为弊漏。

One born without virtuous conduct is as one who has nothing; destroying the proper conduct of men and women — this is called the flaw that oozes.

The outflow (漏) as the operative metaphor: virtue is the vessel; without it, everything leaks away.

生能行善者，此为有所有，其行清净者，是谓无所漏。

生而能行善者，这是有所有，其行为清净者，这称为无所漏。

One born who can perform good conduct has something; one whose conduct is pure — this is called no outflow.

The contrast: virtue as having; pure conduct as the sealed vessel that does not leak.

夫人闻多智，自以为明哲，生疑以自止，是谓痴所弊。

有人听闻很多而自认为聪明，生起疑惑而自我停步，这称为被愚痴所蔽。

A person who hears much and considers themselves wise; generating doubt and stopping themselves — this is called being obscured by delusion.

Intellectual arrogance as a trap: hearing much, concluding one is wise, then becoming stuck in doubt. True wisdom knows its own limits.

悭为秽弊漏，不施为尘垢，胜慢为垢污，不学为大秽。

悭吝是秽弊之漏，不布施是尘垢，慢心是垢污，不学习是大的污秽。

Miserliness is the flowing defilement of filth; not giving is dust and grime; conceit is the stain; not learning is great impurity.

Four named defilements: miserliness, non-generosity, conceit, and not learning. All named impurities or 'dust.'

不诵为尘垢，不勤为尘垢，懈怠为自污，非智慧之道。

不诵习是尘垢，不勤奋是尘垢，懈怠是自我污染，非智慧之道。

Not reciting is dust; not being diligent is dust; laziness is self-contamination — not the path of wisdom.

Learning-related impurities: non-recitation, non-diligence, laziness. These accumulate like dust. The path of wisdom requires active cultivation.

垢中之垢，莫甚于痴，学当舍此，比丘无垢。

垢中之垢，没有比愚痴更甚的，学道者应当舍弃它，如此比丘方无垢。

Of all defilements, none surpasses delusion; one who studies the path should abandon it — thus the monk has no defilement.

Delusion as the supreme defilement: all other defilements are ultimately rooted in not knowing. The monk's goal is its complete abandonment.

苟生无耻者，鸱枭之与鸟，如是厚颜人，杂居反污清。

若人无耻地活着，如猫头鹰与鸟类（混杂），如此厚颜之人，杂居反而污染清净之人。

One who lives shamelessly — like an owl among birds; such a thick-faced person, dwelling mixed with others, contaminates the pure.

The owl among birds: a shameless person in a community of practitioners is like a predator among prey — they contaminate rather than elevate the group.

有耻者易教，无耻者难教，有耻能求善，无耻不能求。

有羞耻心者容易教导，无羞耻心者难以教导，有耻心能求善，无耻心不能求善。

One who has shame is easy to teach; one without shame is difficult to teach; one with shame can seek good; one without shame cannot seek good.

Shame (hiri) as the prerequisite for teachability. Without the capacity to feel shame, improvement becomes impossible — there is no traction for the teaching.

有惭则成法，无惭无所生，能自惭耻者，是谓善法中。

有惭愧则成就法，无惭愧则无所生长，能自我惭耻者，这称为善法之中。

With shame, the Dharma is accomplished; without shame, nothing grows; one who can feel shame — this is called being within good dharmas.

Shame as the root-condition for all virtuous growth. Without it, no Dharma takes hold. With it, all good dharmas are accessible.

起坐不安者，其心常驰驱，诸根悉调顺，为不清净行。

起坐不安稳者，其心常常奔驰，诸根皆调顺，为不清净行。

One who is restless in rising and sitting; whose mind constantly races; even if all faculties are calm — this is impure conduct.

The paradox: external calm alongside inner restlessness is still impure conduct. Both must be settled.

身口意俱净，能护三行者，知此世彼世，是谓有净者。

身、口、意俱清净，能守护三行者，知道此世与彼世（的因果），这称为有净者。

Body, speech, and mind all pure; one who guards these three conducts; knowing this world and the next — this is called the one who has purity.

The complete definition of purity: all three doors (body, speech, mind) purified, and the wisdom to know how action ripples through lives.

观身为净者，以为不净观，如是知正行，到苦际为快。

观身为净者，应以不净观，如此知正行，到达苦的边际为快乐。

One who regards the body as pure should instead contemplate it as impure; knowing correct practice thus, reaching the shore of suffering's end is bliss.

The meditation on bodily impurity as correction: the naturally arising sense that the body is clean and attractive must be deliberately counterbalanced with the impurity contemplation (asubha bhāvanā).

诸根常调顺，如是为梵行，行行若平地，自此彼亦平。

诸根常常调顺，如此是梵行，行于平地，自此处到彼处亦是平坦。

When all faculties are constantly harmonized — this is the holy life; walking on level ground, from here to there is also level.

The harmonized faculties as level ground: the practitioner who has tamed the senses moves through all situations on even footing.

不慢俱等心，慕德贵法者，自无尘垢染，是谓无所有。

不慢、心平等，仰慕德行、珍贵正法者，自无尘垢染，这称为无所有（清净无染）。

Without conceit, with equanimous mind; admiring virtue and valuing the Dharma — oneself without dust-and-grime contamination; this is called having nothing (being undefiled).

Pure mind defined: free of conceit, equanimous, admiring virtue, valuing the Dharma. 'Having nothing' here means no defilement — a kind of poverty that is wealth.

学取正智慧，意以好法乐，除去众不善，是名为清净。

学习取得正智慧，意念以好的法为乐，除去众多不善，这名为清净。

Studying to attain correct wisdom; with the mind delighting in the good Dharma; removing all that is unwholesome — this is called purity.

The practical definition of purity: wisdom + delight in the Dharma + removal of unwholesome things. Three together = pure.

尘垢品终。

尘垢品终。垢中之垢，莫甚于痴；有惭则成法，无惭无所生。身口意俱净，是谓有净者。

End of the chapter on Impurity. Of all defilements, none surpasses delusion. With shame, the Dharma is accomplished; without shame, nothing grows. Body, speech, and mind all pure — this is called having purity.

Closing summary.

总结 · Summary

尘垢品十五颂，以漏（outflow）为喻，善行是器，无善行则处处漏。垢中之垢莫甚于痴；有惭则成法，无惭无所生。身口意俱净为有净者。不净观校正对身体的错觉；诸根调顺则行于平地。清净之道：正智慧、好法为乐、除去不善——三者俱足。

Impurity presents fifteen verses using 'outflow' as the central metaphor — virtue is the vessel; without it, everything leaks away. Delusion is the supreme defilement. Shame (hiri) is the root-condition for all virtuous growth; without it, no Dharma takes hold. Body, speech, and mind all pure — that is the one who has purity. The impurity meditation corrects the body's deceptive attractiveness. Harmonized faculties move through all situations on level ground. Purity: correct wisdom + delight in the Dharma + removal of the unwholesome.
