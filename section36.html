第三十六章 · 泥洹品

法句经 · Dhammapada

← 第三十五章 目录 Contents 第三十七章 →

其于众人中，出家学道者，常修行精进，尽漏得泥洹。泥洹最为乐，佛说无为最，不为有所害，行者入泥洹。若以寂为乐，一切舍善法，于一切世间，此为最上乐。彼岸已度，永不还生，是最寂灭，佛道以安。弃欲不贪，缺无所补，以道学者，佛说离苦。常当学奉持，自依止于法，解脱诸忧患，是谓泥洹乐。度众苦彼岸，以不退转道，心解脱快乐，是谓泥洹喜。一切行无常，苦空非身我，行者以智见，得泥洹快乐。泥洹为常乐，是谓最安隐，于是法常住，是谓得涅槃。诸行无常者，慧眼所观察，苦空无有我，是谓泥洹门。

逐句解释

其于众人中，出家学道者，常修行精进，尽漏得泥洹。

其在众人之中，出家学道者，常修行精进，尽漏而得泥洹（涅槃）。

Among the people, those who leave home to study the path; constantly cultivating with diligence; exhausting the outflows and attaining Nirvana.

The sequence to Nirvana: leaving home, constant cultivation, diligence, exhaustion of the outflows (āsava). The outflows exhausted, Nirvana is attained.

泥洹最为乐，佛说无为最，不为有所害，行者入泥洹。

泥洹是最大的乐，佛说无为是最，不造成任何伤害，行者进入泥洹。

Nirvana is the greatest happiness; the Buddha says non-action is the highest; not causing any harm — the practitioner enters Nirvana.

Nirvana as the greatest happiness — not pleasure but the unconditioned peace beyond all conditioned happiness. Non-action (非为) and harmlessness are its twin gates.

若以寂为乐，一切舍善法，于一切世间，此为最上乐。

若以寂灭为乐，舍去一切（对）善法（的执著），在一切世间中，这是最上的乐。

If one takes quiescence as happiness; releasing all (attachment to) good dharmas; in all the world — this is the highest happiness.

Even good dharmas are released: Nirvana is not the attainment of goodness but the release of all attachment, including attachment to goodness itself.

彼岸已度，永不还生，是最寂灭，佛道以安。

彼岸已度，永不再生，这是最寂灭，佛道而安住。

Having crossed to the other shore; never to be reborn; this is the greatest quiescence; dwelling in peace through the Buddha-path.

Never-returning as the mark of Nirvana. Having crossed, one does not come back. The Buddha-path is what produces and sustains this.

弃欲不贪，缺无所补，以道学者，佛说离苦。

弃欲不贪，（对）缺少无所补充（无执著），以道学者，佛说（能）离苦。

Abandoning desire, not craving; having nothing to make up for lack — one who studies through the path; the Buddha says, departs from suffering.

Contentment as the practical face of Nirvana: no craving, no need to fill what seems lacking. This is departure from suffering in daily life.

常当学奉持，自依止于法，解脱诸忧患，是谓泥洹乐。

常当学奉持，自依止于法，解脱诸忧患，这称为泥洹之乐。

Always study and uphold; take refuge in the Dharma; freed from all worry and suffering — this is called the joy of Nirvana.

The joy of Nirvana is available now: upholding the Dharma, taking refuge in it, freedom from worry and suffering. This is the taste of Nirvana in practice.

度众苦彼岸，以不退转道，心解脱快乐，是谓泥洹喜。

度越众苦彼岸，以不退转之道，心解脱快乐，这称为泥洹之喜。

Crossing the shore of all suffering; through the irreversible path; with a mind liberated and joyful — this is called the joy of Nirvana.

Irreversibility as a mark: the path that leads to Nirvana cannot be reversed. The liberated mind's joy is the experiential signature of arrival.

一切行无常，苦空非身我，行者以智见，得泥洹快乐。

一切行（为）无常，苦空非身我，行者以智慧见，得泥洹快乐。

All formations are impermanent; suffering, empty, not self; the practitioner who sees with wisdom — attains the joy of Nirvana.

The three characteristics again as the direct path to Nirvana: seeing impermanence, suffering, and non-self with wisdom is itself the arrival.

泥洹为常乐，是谓最安隐，于是法常住，是谓得涅槃。

泥洹为常乐，这称为最安隐，于此法常住，这称为得涅槃。

Nirvana is permanent happiness; this is called the most secure; dwelling constantly in this Dharma — this is called attaining Nirvana.

The permanence of Nirvana against the impermanence of all formations. To dwell constantly in the Dharma of Nirvana is to attain it.

诸行无常者，慧眼所观察，苦空无有我，是谓泥洹门。

诸行无常，慧眼所观察，苦空无有我，这称为泥洹门。

All formations are impermanent — what the eye of wisdom observes; suffering, empty, without self — this is called the gate of Nirvana.

The three characteristics are the gate: not obstacles to Nirvana but the very entrance. Seeing them clearly with wisdom's eye is passing through the gate.

总结 · Summary

泥洹品十颂，是法句经中对涅槃最直接的讲述。泥洹最为乐，佛说无为最；一切舍善法，是最上乐；彼岸已度，永不还生；三相是泥洹门——以慧眼观诸行无常、苦、空、无我，就是进入涅槃之门。泥洹为常乐，最安隐，于此法常住，是谓得涅槃。

Nirvana presents ten verses as the T0210's most direct account of Nirvana. Nirvana is the greatest happiness; non-action is highest; even attachment to good dharmas is released for the highest joy. Never returning as the mark of having crossed. The three characteristics are the gate of Nirvana — not obstacles but the very entrance. Seeing impermanence, suffering, emptiness, and non-self with wisdom's eye is passing through the gate. Nirvana is permanent happiness; dwelling constantly in this Dharma is attaining it.
