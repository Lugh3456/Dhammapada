第十八章 · 刀杖品

法句经 · Dhammapada

← 第十七章 目录 Contents 第十九章 →

一切皆惧死，莫不畏杖痛，恕己可为譬，勿杀勿行杖。能常安群生，不加诸楚毒，现世不逢害，后世长安隐。不当粗言，言当畏报，恶往祸来，刀杖归躯。出言以善，如叩钟磬，身无论议，度世则易。欧杖良善，妄谗无罪，其殃十倍，灾迅无赦。生受酷痛，形体毁折，自然恼病，失意恍惚，人所诬咎，或县官厄，财产耗尽，亲戚离别，舍宅所有，灾火焚烧，死入地狱，如是为十。虽裸剪发，长服草衣，沐浴踞石，奈痴结何？不伐杀烧，亦不求胜，人爱天下，所适无怨。世党有人，能知惭愧，是名诱进，如策良马。如策善马，进道能远，人有信戒，定意精进，受道慧成，便灭众苦。自严以修法，灭损受净行，杖不加群生，是沙门道人。无害于天下，终身不遇害，常慈于一切，孰能与为怨？

逐句解释

一切皆惧死，莫不畏杖痛，恕己可为譬，勿杀勿行杖。

一切众生皆畏惧死亡，没有不怕杖痛的，以己度人可作比喻，不要杀生，不要持杖伤人。

All beings fear death; none are without fear of the rod and pain; taking oneself as the measure — do not kill; do not wield the rod.

The golden rule of non-violence: use your own fear of death and pain as the measure of how to treat others.

能常安群生，不加诸楚毒，现世不逢害，后世长安隐。

能够常常安抚群生，不施加苦楚和毒害，此生不遭受伤害，后世长期安隐。

One who always gives security to all beings and does not inflict pain and harm — in the present life does not encounter harm; in future lives enjoys long peace.

Non-harm as protection: the person who gives security to others receives security in return.

不当粗言，言当畏报，恶往祸来，刀杖归躯。

不应当说粗鲁的话，说话应当畏惧果报，恶言往出祸事来至，刀杖之患将归于己身。

Do not speak harshly — speech should be mindful of its consequence; evil goes out and disaster comes; rods and weapons return to one's own body.

The boomerang of harsh speech: harmful words create hostile environments that eventually harm the speaker.

出言以善，如叩钟磬，身无论议，度世则易。

言出以善，如同叩击钟磬（清净响亮），身无可争论，度越世间则容易。

Speaking words that are good — like striking a bell or chime; when the body gives no cause for criticism, crossing the world is easy.

The bell-and-chime image: good speech rings clear and true. The person whose conduct is beyond reproach moves through the world with unusual ease.

欧杖良善，妄谗无罪，其殃十倍，灾迅无赦。

用杖打击善良之人，或妄自诬陷无罪之人，其灾殃十倍于常，灾难迅速且无法宽赦。

Striking good people with rods; falsely slandering the innocent — the calamity is tenfold; disaster comes swiftly and mercilessly.

The escalated consequence of violence against the innocent: harming someone who has done no wrong incurs a disproportionate consequence.

生受酷痛，形体毁折，自然恼病，失意恍惚，人所诬咎，或县官厄，财产耗尽，亲戚离别，舍宅所有，灾火焚烧，死入地狱，如是为十。

此生受尽酷烈痛苦，形体毁损，受恼病，失意恍惚，被人诬陷，遭官府之厄，财产耗尽，亲戚离别，舍宅焚烧，死后入地狱，如此为十种灾殃。

Severe pain in life; the body broken; afflicted by illness; confused in mind; slandered; oppressed by authority; property exhausted; family separated; home destroyed; after death entering hell — these are the ten.

The ten consequences of violence against the good: comprehensive, covering physical, mental, social, material, and posthumous suffering.

虽裸剪发，长服草衣，沐浴踞石，奈痴结何？

即使裸身、剪去头发，长期穿草衣，在石头上沐浴，对愚痴的结缚又有什么用呢？

Though one goes naked, shaves the head, wears grass robes long, bathes on stones — what does this do about the bonds of delusion?

External austerity is insufficient: the most extreme physical renunciation cannot touch the internal bonds of delusion.

不伐杀烧，亦不求胜，人爱天下，所适无怨。

不砍伐、不杀生、不焚烧，也不求胜于人，被天下人所爱，所到之处无有怨仇。

Not cutting, not killing, not burning; not seeking to defeat others — beloved throughout the world; wherever one goes, without enmity.

Non-violence across all domains and non-competitiveness together create a life without enemies.

世党有人，能知惭愧，是名诱进，如策良马。

世间确有这样的人，能够知道惭愧，这叫做被引导进步，如同策动良马。

There are people in the world who know shame and conscience; this is called being drawn toward progress — like spurring a fine horse.

Conscience as the spur: those who feel shame when they do wrong are already in motion toward improvement.

如策善马，进道能远，人有信戒，定意精进，受道慧成，便灭众苦。

如同策动善马，在道路上能够走得很远，人若有信心和戒律，定意精进，受道而慧成，便能灭除众苦。

Like a well-spurred fine horse advancing far — a person with faith and virtue, stillness and diligence, receiving the path and completing wisdom, thereby extinguishes all suffering.

The practitioner equipped with faith, virtue, concentration, and effort covers the entire distance to liberation.

自严以修法，灭损受净行，杖不加群生，是沙门道人。

以自我庄严来修持正法，灭损（烦恼）而受持净行，不以杖击加于群生，这才是沙门道人。

Self-adorned through cultivation of the Dharma; extinguishing faults and undertaking pure conduct; not inflicting rods upon any beings — this is a true monk.

The true monk defined ultimately by non-violence: identified by what they do not inflict, not by what they wear.

无害于天下，终身不遇害，常慈于一切，孰能与为怨？

对天下无有伤害，终身不遭受伤害，常以慈心对待一切，谁能与其结怨？

Harming nothing throughout the world; throughout life not encountering harm; always kind toward everything — who could make an enemy of such a person?

The rhetorical question: genuine universal non-harm makes enmity literally impossible.

总结 · Summary

刀杖品十四颂，以最简洁的逻辑开篇：一切众生皆惧死，以己度人，故勿杀、勿行杖。打伤善人，灾殃十倍；出言以善，如钟磬清音；不伐不杀不烧，所适无怨。真正的沙门以杖不加群生为标志，无害于天下者终身不遇害。

The chapter on Rods and Weapons presents twelve verses on the first principle of Buddhist ethics: non-violence. Beginning with the universal experience of fear as the basis for empathy, it traces the consequences of violence (tenfold calamity) and the fruits of non-violence (universal love, no enmity). The chapter closes with the definition of the true monk as one who inflicts no harm on any being — and the observation that such a person accumulates no enemies.
