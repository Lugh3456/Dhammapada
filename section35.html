第三十五章 · 梵志品

法句经 · Dhammapada

← 第三十四章 目录 Contents 第三十六章 →

截流而渡，无欲如梵，知行已尽，是谓梵志。以无二法，清净渡世，永不复还，是谓梵志。适彼无彼，此彼无有，离尘无染，是谓梵志。观世察明者，不以苦自害，无挂无一法，是谓梵志。无罪如虚空，净无有秽污，以智自调御，是谓梵志。染人所不染，无所不依，离欲断诸漏，是谓梵志。不着于诸欲，心清净无垢，已度生死苦，是谓梵志。弃捐不善者，断除诸烦恼，已度老死苦，是谓梵志。不以贡高慢，解脱心自在，已度诸流者，是谓梵志。持戒无所犯，心净无尘垢，已断诸苦恼，是谓梵志。己身无自欺，亦无欺他人，不以欺自他，是谓梵志。以忍胜无忿，以善胜不善，以施胜悭吝，以实胜虚妄。不以争斗强，是谓梵志律，若以争斗者，是为非梵志。若以绳缚者，智者不知绳，木锁以系者，牢固能坚强。慧人能断爱，见苦以求道，已得超梵界，是谓得梵志。

逐句解释

截流而渡，无欲如梵，知行已尽，是谓梵志。

截断（欲望）流而渡越，无欲如梵天，知行已尽，这称为梵志。

Cutting the stream and crossing over; without desire, like Brahma; knowing that conduct is exhausted — this is called a brahmin.

True brahminhood in three movements: cutting the stream of desire, achieving Brahma-like desirelessness, and knowing that all practice has reached completion.

以无二法，清净渡世，永不复还，是谓梵志。

以无二（不二）之法，清净度越世间，永不再回来，这称为梵志。

Through the non-dual Dharma; purely crossing the world; never returning again — this is called a brahmin.

Non-duality as the vehicle: the brahmin who crosses through the non-dual Dharma does not return to the world of dualisms.

适彼无彼，此彼无有，离尘无染，是谓梵志。

到达彼岸（而）无彼，此岸彼岸皆无，离尘无染，这称为梵志。

Arriving at the other shore and (finding) no other shore; this shore and the other shore both without substance; departing from dust without stain — this is called a brahmin.

The profound teaching: arriving at the other shore only to discover there is no fixed 'other shore.' Both shores dissolve; what remains is unstained presence.

观世察明者，不以苦自害，无挂无一法，是谓梵志。

观世察明者，不以苦自害，无挂无一法，这称为梵志。

One who observes the world and sees clearly; not harming oneself with suffering; with no attachment to any single dharma — this is called a brahmin.

The clear observer: not self-harming through suffering-reactivity, and crucially, not attached to any dharma — even the path itself is not clung to.

无罪如虚空，净无有秽污，以智自调御，是谓梵志。

无罪如虚空，清净无有秽污，以智慧自我调御，这称为梵志。

Without fault like empty space; pure without defilement; with wisdom self-governing — this is called a brahmin.

Purity like space: not a purity achieved by cleansing (which implies prior defilement) but a fundamental purity like that of empty space.

染人所不染，无所不依，离欲断诸漏，是谓梵志。

被染者所不染，无所依赖，离欲断诸漏，这称为梵志。

Not stained by what stains others; without any dependence; departing from desire and severing all outflows — this is called a brahmin.

The brahmin's immunity to stain, independence from all supports, and complete severance of outflows.

不着于诸欲，心清净无垢，已度生死苦，是谓梵志。

不执著于诸欲，心清净无垢，已度越生死苦，这称为梵志。

Not attached to any desires; with a pure and undefiled mind; having crossed the suffering of birth and death — this is called a brahmin.

The crossing accomplished: attachment-free, pure, beyond birth and death. The brahmin has completed the journey.

弃捐不善者，断除诸烦恼，已度老死苦，是谓梵志。

弃捐不善，断除诸烦恼，已度越老死之苦，这称为梵志。

Abandoning and discarding the unwholesome; severing and eliminating all defilements; having crossed the suffering of aging and death — this is called a brahmin.

Action-based brahminhood: it is what one has abandoned (the unwholesome) and eliminated (defilements) that defines the brahmin.

不以贡高慢，解脱心自在，已度诸流者，是谓梵志。

不以贡高慢（傲慢），解脱心自在，已度越诸流者，这称为梵志。

Without arrogance and conceit; with a liberated mind, free; having crossed all streams — this is called a brahmin.

Freedom from conceit as one of the final bonds. The brahmin who has crossed all streams (of craving) has also left arrogance behind.

持戒无所犯，心净无尘垢，已断诸苦恼，是谓梵志。

持戒无所违犯，心净无尘垢，已断除诸苦恼，这称为梵志。

Upholding precepts without violation; with a pure mind, without dust or grime; having severed all suffering and vexation — this is called a brahmin.

The precept-keeping brahmin: not because precepts define brahminhood, but because the brahmin's purity naturally manifests in unviolated precepts.

己身无自欺，亦无欺他人，不以欺自他，是谓梵志。

己身无自欺，也无欺他人，不以欺骗自己和他人，这称为梵志。

Not deceiving oneself; not deceiving others; not using deception toward self or others — this is called a brahmin.

Radical honesty as brahminhood: freedom from deception in both directions — neither self-deception nor other-deception.

以忍胜无忿，以善胜不善，以施胜悭吝，以实胜虚妄。

以忍辱胜过无忿，以善胜不善，以布施胜悭吝，以真实胜虚妄。

Conquering anger through patience; conquering the unwholesome through the wholesome; conquering miserliness through generosity; conquering falsehood through truth.

The four noble substitutions: patience for anger, wholesome for unwholesome, generosity for miserliness, truth for falsehood. The brahmin has accomplished all four.

不以争斗强，是谓梵志律，若以争斗者，是为非梵志。

不以争斗为强，这是梵志的律仪，若以争斗者，这非梵志。

Not using force and conflict to be strong — this is the brahmin's discipline; one who uses force and conflict is not a brahmin.

The brahmin's discipline is defined by non-contention. Strength through force is the opposite of brahminhood.

若以绳缚者，智者不知绳，木锁以系者，牢固能坚强。

若以绳缚者，智者不以绳为缚，以木锁系者，牢固能坚强。

If bound with a rope — the wise do not regard the rope as a bond; bound with a wooden lock, firm and strong.

The inversion of bondage: what appears binding to the unwise (external constraints) is not a bond to the wise. The wooden lock that appears stronger is not necessarily so.

慧人能断爱，见苦以求道，已得超梵界，是谓得梵志。

智慧之人能断爱，见苦以求道，已得超越梵界，这称为得梵志。

The wise person can sever love-craving; seeing suffering, seeking the path; having attained what transcends the Brahma-realm — this is called attaining brahminhood.

The ultimate brahmin transcends even the Brahma-realm. Seeing suffering motivates path-seeking; severing love-craving enables the transcendence.

总结 · Summary

梵志品十五颂，每颂都以「是谓梵志」作结，积累式定义真正的婆罗门。截流而渡，无欲如梵；以无二法清净渡世；到彼岸而无彼，此彼皆无；如虚空无罪，不染众染，断诸漏，度生死苦——这些定义共同描绘出一位在世间而超世间的人。四胜：以忍胜忿，以善胜恶，以施胜悭，以实胜妄。慧人超梵界，是谓得梵志。

The Brahmin presents fifteen verses each ending with 'this is called a brahmin,' building a cumulative portrait of genuine brahminhood. Cutting the stream of desire and crossing over; the non-dual Dharma; arriving at the other shore to find no other shore; purity like empty space; not stained by what stains others; crossing the suffering of birth and death. Four noble substitutions: patience for anger, wholesome for unwholesome, generosity for miserliness, truth for falsehood. The ultimate brahmin transcends even the Brahma-realm.
