第二十四章 · 好喜品

法句经 · Dhammapada

← 第二十三章 目录 Contents 第二十五章 →

以贪欲自蔽，以痴心自覆，求财不知足，是名为好喜。遥见彼城郭，多人所赞叹，有欲以为苦，无欲以为乐。不以乐以苦，不以苦以乐，以乐是谓乐，以苦是谓苦。所谓乐者，非彼以为乐，求乐反得苦，如蛾投灯火。欲我知汝本，意以思想生，我不思想汝，则汝而不有。人知爱欲过，能以理自止，是谓有道智，不为欲所使。见色心不乱，亦复不思惟，是名为少欲，视色无所见。若彼能自制，意不妄思惟，是谓最为善，自制以防欲。意不乐恶法，亦不爱世间，虽处世间中，得脱苦之边。贪欲悉去除，于意无所染，放弃诸不善，此谓修法行。若人能自制，于世无所恋，其心不染著，此人无苦乐。

逐句解释

以贪欲自蔽，以痴心自覆，求财不知足，是名为好喜。

以贪欲遮蔽自己，以痴心覆盖自己，求财而不知足，这名为好喜（执著于乐）。

Concealing oneself with craving; covering oneself with delusion; seeking wealth without knowing contentment — this is called being fond of pleasure.

The opening definition: 'good pleasure' (好喜) in the unwholesome sense means craving + delusion + insatiable seeking. This is what the chapter warns against.

遥见彼城郭，多人所赞叹，有欲以为苦，无欲以为乐。

遥望那城郭，被众多人赞叹，有欲则以（世间）为苦，无欲则以（世间）为乐。

Gazing at that city from afar, praised by many people — with desire the world is suffering; without desire the world is joy.

The same world, two experiences: desire turns it to suffering; its absence turns it to joy. The world has not changed; only the relationship to it.

不以乐以苦，不以苦以乐，以乐是谓乐，以苦是谓苦。

不要以乐来作为苦，不要以苦来作为乐，以乐为乐才是乐，以苦为苦才是苦。

Do not take pleasure as suffering; do not take suffering as pleasure; taking pleasure as pleasure — that is pleasure; taking suffering as suffering — that is suffering.

Clarity about the nature of experience: don't invert reality. This is a warning against both unnecessary austerity (making pleasure into suffering) and delusion (making suffering into pleasure).

所谓乐者，非彼以为乐，求乐反得苦，如蛾投灯火。

所谓的乐，并非彼所以为的乐，求乐反而得苦，如同飞蛾投入灯火。

What is called pleasure is not what they take to be pleasure; seeking pleasure, one obtains suffering instead — like a moth plunging into a flame.

The moth-and-flame image: the attracted creature seeks light (pleasure) and finds destruction. Craving for pleasure produces its opposite.

欲我知汝本，意以思想生，我不思想汝，则汝而不有。

欲啊，我知道你的根本，你是由心意思想而生，我不思想你，你便不存在。

Desire, I know your origin — you arise from mental thought; if I do not think of you, you do not exist.

The Buddha's direct address to Mara/desire: desire has no independent existence. It arises in the thinking mind and can be dismantled by withdrawing attention.

人知爱欲过，能以理自止，是谓有道智，不为欲所使。

人知道爱欲的过患，能以理性自我制止，这称为有道的智慧，不被欲望所役使。

One who knows the faults of love and desire; who can stop themselves through reason — this is called wisdom of the path; not being controlled by desire.

Wisdom of the path: knowing the cost of desire AND having the capacity to stop through reason. Both knowledge and agency are required.

见色心不乱，亦复不思惟，是名为少欲，视色无所见。

见到色（形象）而心不散乱，也不加以思惟执取，这称为少欲，看见色而无所见（不起执著）。

Seeing form without the mind becoming disturbed; also not thinking about it with attachment — this is called few desires; seeing form yet seeing nothing (no grasping).

The training of non-grasping in perception: seeing clearly without the cognitive elaboration that creates attachment. 'Seeing form yet seeing nothing' is the practitioner's gaze.

若彼能自制，意不妄思惟，是谓最为善，自制以防欲。

若彼能自我制御，意不妄加思惟，这称为最善，以自制来防止欲望。

If one can govern oneself, with the mind not thinking randomly — this is called the most good; self-governance to prevent desire.

Self-governance as the highest good: the disciplined mind that does not follow every random thought is the primary defense against desire.

意不乐恶法，亦不爱世间，虽处世间中，得脱苦之边。

意不喜乐恶法，也不爱恋世间，虽然身处世间之中，能够脱离苦的边际。

The mind takes no pleasure in evil dharmas; nor loves the world; even while dwelling in the world — one is liberated from the shore of suffering.

Liberation while in the world: not departure from the world but a changed relationship to it. No delight in evil; no love-clinging to worldly things.

贪欲悉去除，于意无所染，放弃诸不善，此谓修法行。

贪欲悉皆去除，意念无所染污，放弃诸不善，这称为修法行。

With craving entirely removed; with mind-intention unstained; abandoning all unwholesome things — this is called cultivating the path.

The complete definition of path-practice: craving removed, mind uncontaminated, all unwholesomeness abandoned.

若人能自制，于世无所恋，其心不染著，此人无苦乐。

若人能自我制御，对世间无所眷恋，其心不染著，此人无苦乐（超越苦乐）。

If a person can govern themselves; with no attachment to the world; with a mind that has no stain — such a person is beyond suffering and pleasure.

Beyond the duality of suffering and pleasure: the practitioner who has released craving has also released the compulsive oscillation between its opposites.

好喜品终。

好喜品终——知欲如蛾火，以理自制，无苦无乐，得真自在。

End of the chapter on Pleasure — knowing desire as the moth-flame, governing oneself through reason, beyond suffering and pleasure, attaining true freedom.

Summary closing verse.

总结 · Summary

好喜品十一颂，以贪欲自蔽为开篇，以超越苦乐为结语。求乐反得苦，如蛾投灯火；欲以思想生，不思则不有。有道之智，知欲之过而以理自止。见色心不乱，视色无所见——这是少欲的训练。真正的好喜，是脱离苦乐两边的自在。

Pleasure presents eleven verses moving from craving-as-self-concealment to transcendence of the suffering-pleasure duality. Seeking pleasure produces its opposite — like a moth seeking light and finding destruction. Desire arises in the thinking mind; not thinking about it, it ceases to exist. Wisdom of the path means knowing desire's cost AND being able to stop. Seeing form without grasping — 'seeing form, seeing nothing' — is the practitioner's trained gaze. True pleasure is freedom from the oscillation between suffering and pleasure.
