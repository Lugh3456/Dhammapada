第三十二章 · 爱欲品

法句经 · Dhammapada

← 第三十一章 目录 Contents 第三十三章 →

心放在爱欲，见色而止足，及婬染他妇，是为自侵欺。心念爱欲者，出家为邪行，贪淫不谛观，是谓为邪道。爱欲意为田，淫怒痴为种，施戒慧是宝，能令无所有。无欲心解者，无明为所染，虽如虚空净，不以爱污身。见色而不乱，于意无所染，此则无所爱，是谓不受觉。厌离爱欲者，是谓乐苦空，得是三昧者，心解如莲花。已从恩爱出，不与世俗会，恩爱若生时，离之无留意。无子慈无望，父亦无所倚，人不望于斯，彼非爱欲处。不以婬伐人，亦不殊贡高，解脱是无恚，是谓平等行。无欲心离身，从是无有苦，非以爱无苦，乃解脱断缚。

逐句解释

心放在爱欲，见色而止足，及婬染他妇，是为自侵欺。

心放纵于爱欲，见色而满足于此，乃至淫染他人妇，这是自我侵欺。

A mind released into craving and desire; stopping at the sight of form as sufficient; even adulterously defiling another's spouse — this is self-invasion and self-deception.

Craving's progression: first the mind is released into desire, then it stops at visible form, then it crosses into harm. All three stages are self-deception.

心念爱欲者，出家为邪行，贪淫不谛观，是谓为邪道。

心念爱欲者，出家却是邪行，贪淫不正观，这称为邪道。

One whose mind thinks of craving and desire; leaving home is wrong conduct for them; lust and craving without correct contemplation — this is called the wrong path.

The renunciant whose mind remains full of craving is on the wrong path. The robe cannot transform the mind; only correct contemplation does that.

爱欲意为田，淫怒痴为种，施戒慧是宝，能令无所有。

爱欲意为田，淫怒痴为种，布施戒律慧是宝，能令无所有（清净无染）。

The mind of craving and desire is the field; lust, anger, and delusion are the seeds; generosity, precepts, and wisdom are the treasure — able to produce having-nothing (purity).

The agricultural metaphor: the craving-mind is the field, the three roots are the seeds, and virtue is what transforms it. The harvest of virtue is 'having nothing' — pure freedom from defilement.

无欲心解者，无明为所染，虽如虚空净，不以爱污身。

无欲心解脱者，无明所染，虽如虚空清净，不以爱欲污染身。

One whose desireless mind is liberated — though stained by ignorance; though pure as empty space — not defiling the body with craving.

The liberated mind: pure as space, not defiled by craving even though ignorance has left residual traces. This is the ongoing work of practice.

见色而不乱，于意无所染，此则无所爱，是谓不受觉。

见色而不散乱，意念无所染污，此则无所爱，这称为不受觉（不受色的触动）。

Seeing form without becoming disturbed; with mind-intention unstained — thus having no craving; this is called not receiving sense-impressions (not being moved by form).

The trained perceptual response: form is seen but does not disturb, does not stain, does not generate craving. This is 'not receiving' — presence without reactivity.

厌离爱欲者，是谓乐苦空，得是三昧者，心解如莲花。

厌离爱欲者，这称为乐苦空，得到此三昧者，心解脱如莲花。

One who is weary of and departing from craving and desire — this is called delighting-in-suffering-and-emptiness; one who attains this samādhi — the mind liberated like a lotus flower.

The lotus liberation: like the lotus rooted in mud but uncontaminated, the liberated mind lives in the world of craving without being touched by it.

已从恩爱出，不与世俗会，恩爱若生时，离之无留意。

已从恩爱中出离，不与世俗相会，恩爱若生起时，离之而无留意。

Having emerged from love and affection; not meeting with the worldly; when love and affection arise — departing from them without dwelling in mind.

The practitioner's relationship with arising emotions: they are noticed (love and affection arise) and departed from without dwelling. No suppression, no indulgence.

无子慈无望，父亦无所倚，人不望于斯，彼非爱欲处。

无子慈无所希望，父也无所依靠，人不寄望于此，彼处非爱欲之处。

Without children, kindness without expectation; without a father, no dependence; a person does not pin hopes on these things — that is not the place of craving.

Freedom from craving extends to family relations: not the elimination of love but the elimination of expectation, dependence, and hope-pinning.

不以婬伐人，亦不殊贡高，解脱是无恚，是谓平等行。

不以淫欲侵伐他人，也不特别贡高傲慢，解脱于无嗔恚，这称为平等行。

Not attacking others through lust; also not being especially arrogant; liberated through freedom from anger — this is called conduct of equanimity.

Equanimous conduct defined negatively: no lust-driven aggression, no arrogance. The positive: freedom from anger. Together they produce equanimity.

无欲心离身，从是无有苦，非以爱无苦，乃解脱断缚。

无欲之心离于身（执），从此无有苦，不是以爱而无苦，乃是解脱断除缚。

A desireless mind departing from body-attachment — from this, no suffering; not through love is there no suffering, but through liberation and the severing of bonds.

The key distinction: the path to no-suffering is not through love (which creates bonds) but through liberation, which severs them.

爱欲品终。

爱欲品终。爱欲意为田，淫怒痴为种，戒慧为宝；心解如莲花，见色不乱，无欲离身，从是无苦。

End of the chapter on Craving. The craving-mind is the field, lust-anger-delusion are the seeds, precepts and wisdom are the treasure; mind liberated like a lotus, seeing form without disturbance, desireless and departing from body-attachment — from this, no suffering.

Closing summary.

总结 · Summary

爱欲品十颂，以心放爱欲为自侵欺开篇，以解脱断缚无苦结尾。爱欲意为田，淫怒痴为种，施戒慧为宝——农业之喻精准。见色不乱是不受觉；心解如莲花是三昧之果；无欲离身则无苦——非以爱无苦，乃是解脱断缚无苦。

Craving presents ten verses from 'releasing the mind into craving is self-deception' to 'the desireless mind departing from body-attachment has no suffering.' The craving-mind is the field; lust, anger, and delusion are the seeds; virtue and wisdom are the treasure. Seeing form without disturbance is 'not receiving sense-impressions.' The lotus liberation: living in the world of craving without being touched. The path to no-suffering is not through love (which creates bonds) but through liberation, which severs them.
