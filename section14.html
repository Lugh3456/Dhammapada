第十四章 · 明哲品

法句经 · Dhammapada

← 第十三章 目录 Contents 第十五章 →

深观善恶，心知畏忌，畏而不犯，终吉无忧。故世有福，念思绍行，善致其愿，福禄转胜。信善作福，积行不厌，信知阴德，久而必彰。常避无义，不亲愚人，思从贤友，押附上士。喜法卧安，心悦意清，圣人演法，慧常乐行。仁人智者，斋戒奉道，如星中月，照明世间。弓工调角，水人调船，材匠调木，智者调身。譬如厚石，风不能移，智者意重，毁誉不倾。譬如深渊，澄静清明，慧人闻道，心净欢然。大人体无欲，在所照然明，虽或遭苦乐，不高现其智。大贤无世事，不愿子财国，常守戒慧道，不贪邪富贵。智人知动摇，譬如沙中树，朋友志未强，随色染其素。世皆没渊，鲜克度岸，如或有人，欲度必奔。诚贪道者，览受正教，此近彼岸，脱死为上。断五阴法，静思智慧，不反入渊，弃猗其明。抑制情欲，绝乐无为，能自拯济，使意为慧。学取正智，意惟正道，一心受谛，不起为乐，漏尽习除，是得度世。

逐句解释

深观善恶，心知畏忌，畏而不犯，终吉无忧。

深入观察善恶，内心知道畏忌，畏惧而不违犯，终究吉祥无忧。

Deeply observing good and evil; the mind knowing what to fear and avoid; fearing and not transgressing — ultimately auspicious and free from worry.

Moral fear as wisdom: the wise person's restraint is not based on external coercion but on seeing clearly what leads to harm. This seeing produces genuine caution, not anxiety.

故世有福，念思绍行，善致其愿，福禄转胜。

因此世间有福之人，念念思惟而继承修行，善法成就其愿望，福禄越来越殊胜。

Therefore those with merit in the world contemplate and continue the practice; the good fulfils their wishes; merit and blessing grow increasingly excellent.

The compound growth of virtue: each act of good contemplation and practice generates conditions that support the next. Merit is not static but self-reinforcing.

信善作福，积行不厌，信知阴德，久而必彰。

以信心作善积福，积累行持不厌倦，以信心了知阴德（暗中的功德），长久必然彰显。

With faith, doing good and accumulating merit; persisting in practice without weariness; with faith knowing hidden virtue — in time it will certainly manifest.

Hidden virtue that eventually manifests: good action done consistently without expectation of recognition does not disappear — it accumulates and eventually becomes visible.

常避无义，不亲愚人，思从贤友，押附上士。

常常回避无义之事，不亲近愚人，思惟随从贤良的朋友，依附于上等的贤士。

Always avoiding the meaningless; not associating with fools; contemplating following wise friends; attaching oneself to persons of excellence.

The wise person's social ecology: avoid harm, avoid foolishness, seek wisdom, attach to excellence. These choices shape the conditions for continued growth.

喜法卧安，心悦意清，圣人演法，慧常乐行。

以法为乐则安卧，心悦而意识清明，圣人演说正法，有智慧者常以修行为乐。

Delighting in the Dharma, one sleeps peacefully; the mind is glad and consciousness is clear; the noble person proclaims the Dharma; the wise always delight in practice.

Joy in the Dharma is not forced but natural — it produces the ease of conscience that makes sleep undisturbed and waking clear. The noble person's proclamation and the wise person's practice are the social manifestations of this inner joy.

仁人智者，斋戒奉道，如星中月，照明世间。

仁人智者，持斋戒而奉行正道，如同星星中的月亮，照耀世间。

The kind person and the wise, keeping the precepts and following the path — like the moon among stars, they illuminate the world.

The moon among stars: brightness is relative, but the truly wise person's quality is categorically higher, like the moon's light compared to starlight.

弓工调角，水人调船，材匠调木，智者调身。

弓匠调整弓角，水手调驾船，木匠调整木材，智者调伏自身。

The bow-maker shapes the horn; the sailor steers the boat; the carpenter works the wood; the wise person tames the self.

The craftsman metaphor for self-cultivation: each craft requires expertise and patient adjustment. The self is the wise person's material — requiring the same skill and care.

譬如厚石，风不能移，智者意重，毁誉不倾。

就像厚重的磐石，风吹不能移动，智者意志沉重，毁誉不能使其倾倒。

Like a massive rock that wind cannot move — the wise person's will is heavy; praise and blame do not tip it.

The rock in the wind: a natural standard for equanimity. Not unmoved in the sense of unawareness, but unable to be destabilized by the forces that topple lighter things.

譬如深渊，澄静清明，慧人闻道，心净欢然。

就像深深的水渊，澄清静寂而清明，有智慧的人听闻正道，心净而欢然。

Like a deep pool, settled, still and clear — the wise person hearing the Dharma finds the mind pure and glad.

The deep pool: not stagnant but settled. The surface of still water reflects clearly what is above it. The settled mind that hears the Dharma sees it accurately and responds with gladness.

大人体无欲，在所照然明，虽或遭苦乐，不高现其智。

大人的体性没有欲望，所在之处光明照耀，虽然遭遇苦乐，也不高扬显示其智慧。

The great person's nature is without desire; wherever they are, clarity shines; though meeting suffering and pleasure, they do not flaunt their wisdom.

The non-display of wisdom: the truly wise do not advertise their attainment. The illumination is real and pervasive, but it is not performed.

大贤无世事，不愿子财国，常守戒慧道，不贪邪富贵。

大贤者没有世间事务的牵绊，不贪愿子女、财富和国土，常守戒律、智慧之道，不贪求邪的富贵。

The great wise person has no worldly entanglements; they do not desire children, wealth, or a kingdom; always keeping to the path of virtue and wisdom; not craving ill-gotten wealth or status.

Freedom from worldly entanglement is not ascetic rejection but clarity of priority. The great wise person is not prohibited from these things — they simply do not need them.

智人知动摇，譬如沙中树，朋友志未强，随色染其素。

智者知道动摇不定，就像沙中的树（根基不稳），朋友的志向未坚强，便随着环境染色其本质。

The wise know what is unstable — like a tree in sand; a friend whose will is not strong is dyed by circumstances, losing their original colour.

Trees in sand have no grip: superficially standing but easily toppled. Friends without strong conviction are similarly vulnerable to the coloring of whatever environment they enter.

世皆没渊，鲜克度岸，如或有人，欲度必奔。

世间众生都沉没于苦海，很少有人能度到彼岸，如果有人想度越，必定要勇猛奔赴。

The world is mostly submerged in the abyss; few can cross to the farther shore; but if someone wishes to cross, they must press forward vigorously.

The sobering statistics of liberation: most people are submerged, and reaching the other shore is rare. But the verse does not end in despair — it ends in vigorous effort. The rare crossing is possible.

诚贪道者，览受正教，此近彼岸，脱死为上。

真诚渴求道的人，广泛接受正教，这接近彼岸，脱离死亡是最殊胜的。

One who sincerely craves the path; widely receiving the correct teaching — this draws near to the farther shore; liberation from death is supreme.

Sincere craving for the path, combined with wide reception of correct teaching, constitutes the condition for crossing. The craving that usually harms becomes the energy of liberation when redirected toward the Dharma.

断五阴法，静思智慧，不反入渊，弃猗其明。

断除五阴之法，静思中产生智慧，不再返回深渊，放弃依倚而入于光明。

Cutting the five aggregates' dharmas; wisdom arising in stillness; not returning to the abyss; abandoning reliance and entering brightness.

The five aggregates (form, feeling, perception, mental formations, consciousness) are the components of what we take to be self. Cutting their grip is the deepest form of liberation.

抑制情欲，绝乐无为，能自拯济，使意为慧。

抑制情欲，断绝（对世间）乐的执著而住于无为，能够自我救济，使心意成为智慧。

Suppressing sensual desires; cutting off attachment to pleasure for the unconditioned; able to save oneself; turning the mind into wisdom.

The self-rescue: no one else can do this for us. The mind that turns itself toward wisdom — rather than being used by desire — is the agent of its own liberation.

学取正智，意惟正道，一心受谛，不起为乐，漏尽习除，是得度世。

修学获取正智，心意思惟正道，一心接受真谛，以不生起（烦恼）为乐，漏尽习除，这才是得以度越世间。

Learning and obtaining right wisdom; the mind contemplating the right path; one-pointed reception of truth; non-arising as joy; taints exhausted and habits removed — this is what it means to cross the world.

The completion sequence: right wisdom → right path → one-pointed truth → non-arising → exhaustion of taints → liberation. Non-arising as joy is a striking formulation: the absence of compulsive arising is itself pleasurable, not merely neutral.

总结 · Summary

明哲品十七颂，描绘真正的智者：磐石般不为毁誉所动，深渊般澄静清明，星中月般照耀世间。智者调身如匠人调材，深观善恶，积累阴德，不贪世事，以不生起烦恼为乐。品末以'漏尽习除，是得度世'作结，揭示一切修行的最终归宿：非生起，即解脱。

The chapter on the Wise presents seventeen verses portraying genuine wisdom in action: equanimous as a rock, clear as a deep pool, illuminating like the moon among stars. The wise person tames the self as a craftsman tames material — with skill, patience, and direct knowledge. The chapter traces the path from deep observation of good and evil through to the complete exhaustion of taints, with non-arising as joy described as the final and deepest peace.
