第三十七章 · 生死品

法句经 · Dhammapada

← 第三十六章 目录 Contents 第三十八章 →

命如花果熟，常恐会零落，已生皆有死，此是凡夫法。诸行无常，是生灭法，生灭灭已，寂灭为乐。人命不可称，往来转已疾，少壮至老死，光影常驰走。命欲日夜尽，及时可勤学，无常来迫速，是日已过去。生时无聚积，不以财自亲，当知此灭尽，死多弃捐时。亦有人生死，世间有终始，学者求法智，是为正解知。不知从何起，展转无所趣，生死不知根，此为苦薮。

逐句解释

命如花果熟，常恐会零落，已生皆有死，此是凡夫法。

命如花果熟透，常常恐惧将会凋零，已生皆有死，这是凡夫之法。

Life is like a flower or fruit that has ripened — constantly fearing it will fall; all who have been born will die; this is the dharma of ordinary beings.

The ripened fruit as the image of life at its peak: beautiful and at precisely that moment closest to falling. The dharma of ordinary beings is birth followed by death.

诸行无常，是生灭法，生灭灭已，寂灭为乐。

诸行无常，这是生灭之法，生灭灭已，寂灭为乐。

All formations are impermanent — this is the law of arising and ceasing; when arising and ceasing have ceased — quiescence is happiness.

Perhaps the most celebrated verse of the Pali Dhammapada's counterpart: impermanence → arising-and-ceasing → when the arising-and-ceasing itself ceases → quiescence. Nirvana is not the end of things but the end of the compulsive cycle.

人命不可称，往来转已疾，少壮至老死，光影常驰走。

人的寿命不可测量，往来转换已然迅疾，从少壮至老死，光影常常驰走。

Human life cannot be measured; the going and coming transforms swiftly; from youth to aging and death — light and shadow are always racing.

Light and shadow racing: an image of time's speed. Youth and aging are not separate stages but continuous motion — light already becoming shadow.

命欲日夜尽，及时可勤学，无常来迫速，是日已过去。

命欲日夜竭尽，及时可勤学，无常来迫速，这一日已经过去。

Life is exhausted day and night; while there is time, one can diligently study; impermanence comes pressing swiftly — this day has already passed.

The urgency of practice: each day that passes is irretrievable. Impermanence does not wait. The time for diligent study is now, before it passes.

生时无聚积，不以财自亲，当知此灭尽，死多弃捐时。

生时无所聚积，不以财货自亲，当知此（身）将灭尽，死时多有舍弃。

While living, without accumulating; not becoming intimate with wealth; know that this (body) will perish — at death much will be abandoned.

The inventory of death: nothing accumulated (spiritually), no genuine closeness with wealth, the body perishes, everything material is left behind.

亦有人生死，世间有终始，学者求法智，是为正解知。

也有人（知）生死，世间有终始，学者求法与智，这是正确的了解知见。

There are also those who understand birth and death; the world has its end and beginning; one who studies, seeking the Dharma and wisdom — this is correct understanding and knowledge.

Counter to the ordinary being lost in the birth-death cycle: the one who understands it and seeks wisdom through the Dharma. Correct understanding is the first step out.

不知从何起，展转无所趣，生死不知根，此为苦薮。

不知从何处起，辗转无所归趣，生死不知其根，这是苦的薮泽（苦的丛林）。

Not knowing where it begins; turning over and over without direction; not knowing the root of birth and death — this is the thicket of suffering.

The thicket of suffering: the person who does not know the root of birth-and-death wanders without direction in dense suffering. Knowing the root is the beginning of escape.

生死品终。

生死品终。命如花果熟，生灭灭已寂灭为乐；及时勤学，无常来迫速；知生死之根，出苦薮泽。

End of the chapter on Birth and Death. Life is like a ripened flower-fruit; when arising-and-ceasing ceases, quiescence is happiness; diligently study while there is time, impermanence comes swiftly; know the root of birth and death — emerge from the thicket of suffering.

Closing summary.

总结 · Summary

生死品七颂，以命如花果熟开篇，以苦薮泽（不知生死之根的迷失）结尾。最著名的颂：「诸行无常，是生灭法，生灭灭已，寂灭为乐」——当生灭本身止息，寂灭即乐。光影驰走、无常迫速，及时勤学是唯一的出路。知生死根，出苦薮。

Birth and Death presents seven verses from 'life like a ripened fruit' to 'the thicket of suffering for those who do not know the root of birth-and-death.' The most celebrated verse: 'all formations are impermanent — when arising-and-ceasing have ceased, quiescence is happiness.' Nirvana is not the end of things but the end of the compulsive cycle. Light and shadow are always racing; impermanence does not wait. Knowing the root of birth-and-death is the beginning of escape from the thicket.
