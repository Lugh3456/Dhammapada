第二十七章 · 奉持品

法句经 · Dhammapada

← 第二十六章 目录 Contents 第二十八章 →

奉持法以善，自以为道宝，学法无有忧，愍哀如父母。始学寡恩义，而强为他役，先身自作证，乃学为人长。法本法无法，无法法亦法，今付无法时，法法何曾法？奉行以善法，行义不失道，身意常安宁，不如言取道。若诵法已，忘失于本，是为无行，若能不忘，是为有行。能解经义者，是为最胜道，行道得一切，远离于恶法。贤者能自察，自察其行者，不造罪法事，于此得解脱。一切爱行者，能自观心已，意以为第一，知足能持戒。学法知止足，不为利失道，不见时不知，是谓不持律。

逐句解释

奉持法以善，自以为道宝，学法无有忧，愍哀如父母。

奉持正法以善，自以道为宝，学法则无有忧，如父母般的哀愍。

Upholding the Dharma through goodness; taking the path as one's treasure; studying the Dharma has no worry; with compassion like a parent's.

Observance of the Dharma transforms the practitioner's fundamental orientation: the path becomes treasure, worry dissolves, compassion becomes parental.

始学寡恩义，而强为他役，先身自作证，乃学为人长。

初学时（对他人）少恩义，而勉强为他人服务，先自身作证（自修），乃能学习做众人的长者。

At the start of learning, with few kindnesses to others, and forcing oneself to serve others — first self-verify through one's own body; then one can learn to be an elder for others.

The realistic arc of practice: early on, kindness is limited and service is an effort. Self-verification comes first; only after genuine attainment can one truly guide others.

法本法无法，无法法亦法，今付无法时，法法何曾法？

法本来无法，无法也是法，今以无法（的法）付予，法法又何曾是法？

The Dharma's root: the Dharma has no Dharma; no-Dharma is also Dharma; now entrusted in the time of no-Dharma — the Dharma of dharmas, when was it ever Dharma?

One of the most profound verses of the T0210: the teaching of emptiness applied to itself. The Dharma, properly understood, is not a fixed thing — it points beyond itself.

奉行以善法，行义不失道，身意常安宁，不如言取道。

奉行善法，行义而不失道，身意常安宁，不以言语执取道。

Upholding and practicing good dharmas; acting righteously without losing the path; body and mind constantly peaceful — not grasping the path through words.

The path is not in the words: good conduct and righteousness produce peace, but the path itself cannot be grasped conceptually through language.

若诵法已，忘失于本，是为无行，若能不忘，是为有行。

若诵习法后，遗忘了根本，这是无行，若能不忘，这是有行。

If after reciting the Dharma one forgets the root — this is conduct-less; if one does not forget — this is having conduct.

Recitation without retention of the essential is hollow. The test of Dharma-learning is whether the root meaning has been absorbed and retained.

能解经义者，是为最胜道，行道得一切，远离于恶法。

能了解经义者，是为最胜道，行道得一切，远离于恶法。

One who can understand the meaning of the sutras — this is the most excellent path; practicing the path, one attains everything; departing far from evil dharmas.

Understanding the meaning (not just memorizing the words) is the supreme Dharma-practice. From it flows all attainment and the natural departure from evil.

贤者能自察，自察其行者，不造罪法事，于此得解脱。

贤者能够自我审察，自我审察其行为者，不造作罪法之事，于此得到解脱。

The wise can examine themselves; one who examines their own conduct — not creating harmful actions; thereby attaining liberation.

Self-examination as the mechanism of liberation: the habit of honest self-inquiry prevents harmful actions before they arise.

一切爱行者，能自观心已，意以为第一，知足能持戒。

一切爱好修行者，能自我观察心已，意念为第一，知足能持戒。

All who love practice can observe their own minds; with mind-intention as the first priority; knowing contentment and able to uphold precepts.

The practitioner's program: mind-observation, mind-as-first-priority, contentment, precept-keeping. These four together define the lover of practice.

学法知止足，不为利失道，不见时不知，是谓不持律。

学法知足而止，不为利益而失道，不见时机而不知，这称为不持律。

Studying the Dharma and knowing when to stop; not losing the path for the sake of gain; not knowing the time and not knowing — this is called not upholding the precepts.

Three failures of discipline: not knowing enough, being led astray by gain, and lacking discernment of timing. Each undermines precept-practice.

奉持品终。

奉持品终。法本无法，奉行以善，能解经义是最胜道，贤者自察而得解脱。

End of the chapter on Observance. The Dharma's root is no-Dharma; uphold it through goodness; understanding sutra-meaning is the most excellent path; the wise examine themselves and attain liberation.

Closing summary.

总结 · Summary

奉持品九颂，以奉持法善、以道为宝开篇。法本无法，无法亦法——最深刻的空性教义贯穿其中。能解经义是最胜道；诵法忘本是无行；贤者自察不造罪而得解脱。奉持品的核心：理解意义，自我审察，知足持戒，不为利益失道。

Observance presents nine verses on upholding the Dharma through understanding, not mere recitation. The most profound verse: 'the Dharma's root is no-Dharma; no-Dharma is also Dharma' — emptiness applied to the Dharma itself. Understanding sutra-meaning is the most excellent path; reciting without retaining the root is conduct-less. The wise examine themselves, do not create harm, and attain liberation. Contentment, mind-as-first-priority, and precept-keeping define the practitioner.
