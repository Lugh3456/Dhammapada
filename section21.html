第二十一章 · 世俗品

法句经 · Dhammapada

← 第二十章 目录 Contents 第二十二章 →

受辱心如地，行忍如门阈，净如水无垢，生尽无彼受。若见善知识，能成明智慧，归命如来者，必得度苦际。不失人信义，行在世无怯，近者受其利，死而上天堂。能常以慈心，奉事于尊长，亦常念众生，此是诸佛教。明智信具足，是为最吉祥，所愿皆成就，乘是道彼岸。世间有威福，智者所不知，是以真谛观，世间虚妄法。见彼曲秽污，亦见其清净，当知彼往来，说不可穷尽。世间有三法，明者所思惟，苦尽解脱路，佛以为福地。夫求安隐者，当舍不净法，见法分别义，善修行梵行。若知世有乐，亦知其无乐，贪欲不染身，是名为贤明。慎勿信汝意，意终不可信，慎勿与色会，色会即祸生。人随情欲走，如流运自然，能以法制意，是应圣人道。了知世间事，如幻如梦寐，不令心驰荡，是应住解脱。

逐句解释

受辱心如地，行忍如门阈，净如水无垢，生尽无彼受。

受到侮辱时心如大地（广阔承载），行忍辱如同门槛（坚固），洁净如水无有垢染，生命终结而彼受（苦）不再。

A mind like the earth when receiving insult; practicing patience like a threshold; pure as water, without defilement — when life ends, that suffering is no longer experienced.

Three images of patience under insult: the earth (receives everything without resentment), the threshold (endures constant traffic), water (pure and unstained). The reward is the end of suffering.

若见善知识，能成明智慧，归命如来者，必得度苦际。

若能见到善知识，能够成就明智慧，归命于如来的人，必定能够度脱苦的边际。

If one encounters a good spiritual friend, one can accomplish bright wisdom; one who takes refuge in the Tathāgata necessarily crosses beyond the shore of suffering.

The spiritual friend (kalyāṇamitta) as the proximate cause of wisdom; taking refuge as the culminating act that ensures crossing.

不失人信义，行在世无怯，近者受其利，死而上天堂。

不失去人的信义，行于世间无所畏惧，亲近者受其利益，死后升上天堂。

Not losing human trust and righteousness; moving through the world without fear; those who approach receive benefit; after death ascending to the heavens.

Integrity (trustworthiness + righteousness) yields three fruits: personal fearlessness, benefit to those nearby, and positive rebirth.

能常以慈心，奉事于尊长，亦常念众生，此是诸佛教。

能够常以慈心，恭奉侍事于长者，也常常念及众生，这是诸佛的教导。

Always able to maintain loving-kindness; respectfully serving elders; always keeping beings in mind — this is the teaching of all Buddhas.

The universal Buddha-teaching condensed: loving-kindness, respect for elders, and constant awareness of all beings.

明智信具足，是为最吉祥，所愿皆成就，乘是道彼岸。

明智和信心具足，这是最大的吉祥，所愿皆得成就，乘此道到达彼岸。

Wisdom and faith fully accomplished — this is the supreme blessing; all wishes are fulfilled; riding this path, one reaches the other shore.

The convergence of wisdom and faith as the greatest blessing, leading directly to the other shore of liberation.

世间有威福，智者所不知，是以真谛观，世间虚妄法。

世间有威势和福报，智者对此不知（不执著），因此以真谛观，（知）世间是虚妄之法。

The world has prestige and blessings — the wise do not (cling to) this; therefore, viewing through ultimate truth, the world's dharmas are seen as illusory.

The wise person's relationship to worldly success: not ignorant of it, but not attached. Through ultimate-truth vision, all worldly things are seen as illusory.

见彼曲秽污，亦见其清净，当知彼往来，说不可穷尽。

见到那弯曲的污秽，也见到其清净，当知彼（众生的）往来，说来不可穷尽。

Seeing that which is crooked and defiled; also seeing what is pure; knowing that the coming and going of beings is inexhaustible — speaking of it cannot reach an end.

The breadth of reality: both the defiled and the pure are seen. The cycle of rebirth is so vast it cannot be fully described.

世间有三法，明者所思惟，苦尽解脱路，佛以为福地。

世间有三种法，明者加以思惟，苦尽解脱之路，佛以此为福地。

The world has three dharmas the wise contemplate; the path where suffering ends and liberation occurs — the Buddha regards this as blessed ground.

The three dharmas: likely the three characteristics (impermanence, suffering, non-self). The path of liberation is the blessed ground.

夫求安隐者，当舍不净法，见法分别义，善修行梵行。

求安稳者，应当舍弃不净之法，见法分别其义，善好修行梵行。

One who seeks peace and security should abandon impure dharmas; see the Dharma and discern its meaning; skillfully cultivate the holy life.

Three requirements for peace: abandon impurity, understand the Dharma, cultivate the holy life. All three together.

若知世有乐，亦知其无乐，贪欲不染身，是名为贤明。

若知世间有乐，也知世间无常乐，贪欲不染污身心，这称为贤明。

Knowing that the world has pleasure; also knowing the world has no lasting pleasure; with craving not staining body and mind — this is called wisdom.

The dual knowing: pleasure exists (not denied) and it is impermanent (not idealized). This balanced knowing, combined with non-attachment, constitutes wisdom.

慎勿信汝意，意终不可信，慎勿与色会，色会即祸生。

谨慎地不要相信你的意（念），意念终究不可完全相信，谨慎地不要与色（欲）相会，相会则祸事生起。

Be careful not to trust your thoughts — thoughts cannot ultimately be trusted; be careful not to meet with sensual pleasures — meeting with them, disaster arises.

Practical warnings: don't trust undisciplined thoughts, don't pursue sensory gratification. Both paths lead to trouble.

人随情欲走，如流运自然，能以法制意，是应圣人道。

人随着情欲走，如同水流自然运转，能以正法制御意念，这才符合圣人之道。

People follow emotions and desires as naturally as water flows; one who can govern the mind through the Dharma — this accords with the path of the sage.

The contrast: most people follow desire as naturally as water flows downhill; the sage reverses the current through Dharma-governed mind.

了知世间事，如幻如梦寐，不令心驰荡，是应住解脱。

了知世间之事，如幻如梦，不令心驰荡（散乱），这才应当安住于解脱。

Knowing thoroughly that worldly matters are like illusion, like dreams — not letting the mind gallop and scatter — this is what it means to abide in liberation.

Liberation is not elsewhere: it is here, in the mind that sees through illusion and does not scatter. This is the final teaching of the chapter.

世俗品终，知世如幻，慎守其意。

世俗品终。知世如幻，谨慎守护其意。

End of the chapter on the World: know the world as illusion; guard the mind with care.

Summary verse.

总结 · Summary

世俗品十三颂，以受辱忍辱开篇，以知世如幻结尾。善知识成就智慧，信义行于无怯，慈心奉事长者——这些是世间行道之基。世间有威福，智者以真谛观之，皆属虚妄。随欲如水流，以法制意方是圣人道。知世如幻，不令心驰，是为解脱安住。

The World presents thirteen verses moving from patience under insult to knowing the world as illusion. Good spiritual friends, integrity, loving-kindness toward elders — these are the foundations of practice in the world. Worldly prestige and blessings, seen through ultimate truth, are illusory. Most people follow desires as naturally as water flows; the sage reverses this through Dharma-governed mind. Knowing the world as illusion, keeping the mind from scattering — this is abiding in liberation.
