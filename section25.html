第二十五章 · 忿怒品

法句经 · Dhammapada

← 第二十四章 目录 Contents 第二十六章 →

忿怒不见法，忿怒不知道，能除忿怒者，福喜常随身。贪淫不见法，愚痴不知道，除贪淫愚痴，是则为最胜。慧以见道，亦以觉意，离忿怒痴，得漏尽道。胜怒以不怒，胜恶以善行，胜贪以布施，胜欺以真实。不欺不怒，意不多求，如是三事，死则上天。常自摄身者，不以苦为苦，是谓最安隐，无逼迫自强。忿怒为毒本，忿怒毒中箭，能以智拔箭，忿怒则自安。先当正其身，次当正其心，身心皆已正，道业乃得成。以忍得无忿，以戒得无恼，以慧断诸漏，以道住无为。

逐句解释

忿怒不见法，忿怒不知道，能除忿怒者，福喜常随身。

忿怒则不见法，忿怒则不知道，能除去忿怒者，福与喜常随身。

Anger does not see the Dharma; anger does not know the path; one who can remove anger — blessings and joy constantly follow.

Anger's double blindness: it obscures both the Dharma (truth) and the path (practice). Removing anger restores both sight and joy.

贪淫不见法，愚痴不知道，除贪淫愚痴，是则为最胜。

贪淫则不见法，愚痴则不知道，除去贪、淫、愚痴，这是最胜。

Lust does not see the Dharma; delusion does not know the path; removing lust and delusion — this is the most excellent.

The three roots of unskilfulness (greed, lust, delusion) are named. Each has the same effect as anger: blindness to truth and path.

慧以见道，亦以觉意，离忿怒痴，得漏尽道。

智慧可以见到道，也可以使意念觉悟，离忿怒和愚痴，得漏尽之道。

Wisdom sees the path; wisdom also awakens the mind; departing from anger and delusion — one attains the path of exhausting the outflows.

Wisdom as the antidote: it both sees the path and awakens the mind, and its application to anger and delusion leads to arahantship.

胜怒以不怒，胜恶以善行，胜贪以布施，胜欺以真实。

以不怒胜怒，以善行胜恶，以布施胜贪，以真实胜欺。

Conquer anger through non-anger; conquer evil through good conduct; conquer greed through generosity; conquer deception through truth.

The four noble conquests: each defilement overcome not by suppression but by its positive opposite. Non-anger is not mere calm but active non-retaliation.

不欺不怒，意不多求，如是三事，死则上天。

不欺骗不忿怒，意念不多求，如此三事，死后升上天界。

Not deceiving, not angering, not craving excessively in the mind — these three things: after death, ascending to the heavens.

Three practices sufficient for positive rebirth: truth-telling, non-anger, and contentment of mind. Simple but demanding.

常自摄身者，不以苦为苦，是谓最安隐，无逼迫自强。

常常自我摄持身心者，不以苦为苦，这称为最安隐，无逼迫而自强。

One who always restrains body and mind does not experience suffering as suffering; this is called the most secure — without compulsion, self-strengthening.

The trained practitioner's relationship to suffering: not that suffering disappears, but that the reactivity to it subsides. Self-strengthening without being compelled.

忿怒为毒本，忿怒毒中箭，能以智拔箭，忿怒则自安。

忿怒是毒的根本，忿怒是毒中之箭，能以智慧拔出此箭，忿怒则自然安息。

Anger is the root of poison; anger is an arrow of poison; one who can with wisdom pull out this arrow — anger then naturally rests.

The poison-arrow metaphor from the Māluṅkyaputta Sutta adapted: anger itself is the arrow lodged in us, and wisdom is what removes it.

先当正其身，次当正其心，身心皆已正，道业乃得成。

先当端正其身，次当端正其心，身心皆已端正，道业乃得成就。

First straighten the body; next straighten the mind; when body and mind are both straight — the work of the path is accomplished.

Sequence matters: body before mind. Physical conduct comes first, then mental alignment. Both together accomplish the path.

以忍得无忿，以戒得无恼，以慧断诸漏，以道住无为。

以忍辱得无忿，以戒律得无恼，以智慧断诸漏，以道住无为。

Through patience, attaining freedom from anger; through precepts, freedom from vexation; through wisdom, severing the outflows; through the path, abiding in non-action.

Four attainments through four practices: patience→non-anger, precepts→non-vexation, wisdom→outflows severed, path→non-action. Complete and self-sufficient.

忿怒品终。

忿怒品终。以不怒胜怒，以善胜恶，以布施胜贪，以真实胜欺——四胜道，心无毒箭。

End of the chapter on Anger. Conquer anger through non-anger, evil through good, greed through generosity, deception through truth — the four noble conquests, the mind free of the poison-arrow.

Closing summary verse.

总结 · Summary

忿怒品九颂，以忿怒令人不见法开篇，以忍戒慧道四种对治结尾。忿怒为毒本，忿怒毒中箭，智慧拔箭则自安。以不怒胜怒，以善胜恶，以布施胜贪，以真实胜欺——四胜道不靠压制，而靠以正向力量替代。身正心正，道业乃成。

Anger presents nine verses from 'anger blinds one to the Dharma' to the four antidotes: patience, precepts, wisdom, and the path. Anger is both root-poison and poison-arrow; wisdom pulls it out. The four noble conquests overcome each defilement with its positive opposite — non-anger, good conduct, generosity, truth. Not suppression but transformation. Body first, then mind: both straight, the path is accomplished.
