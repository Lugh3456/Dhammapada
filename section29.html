第二十九章 · 广衍品

法句经 · Dhammapada

← 第二十八章 目录 Contents 第三十章 →

精进得无为，怠退若死人，志向为上者，天龙所护念。以爱乐精进，以修行佛语，不以放逸退，是谓广大行。有戒德闻者，广布多闻人，少欲而精进，多知广成就。志慕如来者，出家不放逸，以法顺解脱，是谓广衍行。无忧无所畏，亦不择好恶，学者当奉持，此道最上行。多闻能持固，奉法为垣墙，精进难逾越，是谓广大人。比丘立是法，从此多有果，一往不返还，速令得苦际。若人行广道，自以修七法，名誉以远至，三界无所著。

逐句解释

精进得无为，怠退若死人，志向为上者，天龙所护念。

精进则得无为，懈怠退堕如死人，志向上进者，天龙所护念。

Through diligence one attains non-action (the unconditioned); through laziness one retreats and is like a dead person; one with aspirations directed upward — is guarded and minded by the heavens and dragons.

Non-action (无为) as the fruit of diligence — not passivity but the effortless state beyond conditioned striving. Laziness is spiritual death. Aspiration draws protection.

以爱乐精进，以修行佛语，不以放逸退，是谓广大行。

以爱乐而精进，以修行佛语，不以放逸退步，这称为广大行。

With joy and delight being diligent; cultivating the Buddha's words; not retreating through heedlessness — this is called broad and great practice.

Broad cultivation defined: diligence motivated by joy (not fear), anchored in the Buddha's teaching, protected against heedlessness.

有戒德闻者，广布多闻人，少欲而精进，多知广成就。

有戒德而闻者，广布（善法）于多闻者，少欲而精进，多知广泛成就。

One with moral virtue and learning; spreading (the Dharma) widely among those of much hearing; with few desires and diligent; knowing much and achieving broadly.

The bodhisattva-like figure who combines virtue, learning, restraint, and diligence to spread the Dharma broadly among the learned.

志慕如来者，出家不放逸，以法顺解脱，是谓广衍行。

志慕如来者，出家而不放逸，以法顺解脱，这称为广衍行。

One who aspires after the Tathāgata; leaving home without heedlessness; following the Dharma toward liberation — this is called broad cultivation.

Three marks of the genuine renunciant: aspiration for the Tathāgata's qualities, consistent non-heedlessness, and Dharma-following toward liberation.

无忧无所畏，亦不择好恶，学者当奉持，此道最上行。

无忧无所畏，也不分别好恶，学者应当奉持，这道是最上行。

Without worry, without fear; not discriminating good and bad; one who studies should uphold this — this path is the most excellent practice.

Equanimity as non-discrimination: not indifference but the cessation of compulsive like-dislike reactions. This is the most excellent practice.

多闻能持固，奉法为垣墙，精进难逾越，是谓广大人。

多闻能持固，奉法为垣墙，精进难逾越，这称为广大人。

Much hearing can hold firmly; taking the Dharma as a surrounding wall; diligence is difficult to surpass — this is called a broad and great person.

The fortified practitioner: much learning as the foundation, the Dharma as the surrounding wall, diligence as the impossible-to-breach defense.

比丘立是法，从此多有果，一往不返还，速令得苦际。

比丘立此法，从此多有果实，一往不返还，迅速令其得苦的边际。

A monk establishing this Dharma — from this, there are many fruits; going forward without returning — quickly reaching the shore of suffering's end.

One-way journey: the practitioner who establishes genuine Dharma moves steadily forward without retreating, and reaches liberation quickly.

若人行广道，自以修七法，名誉以远至，三界无所著。

若人行广道，自以修七法，名誉由此远至，三界无所执著。

If a person walks the broad path; through cultivating the seven factors (of awakening); reputation reaches far; in the three realms, no attachment.

The seven factors of awakening (七觉支) as the specific content of 'broad cultivation.' Their practice generates renown and non-attachment across all realms.

广衍品终。

广衍品终。精进则得无为，以法为墙，以爱乐修行，广大人无著三界。

End of the chapter on Broad Cultivation. Through diligence one attains non-action; with the Dharma as surrounding wall; practicing with joy — the broad and great person has no attachment in the three realms.

Closing summary.

总结 · Summary

广衍品八颂，以精进得无为开篇，以七觉支三界无著结尾。广大行：以爱乐精进，以佛语为根，不以放逸退步。广大人：多闻持固，奉法为墙，精进难逾越。一往不返，速得苦际——这是广衍品的修行节奏。

Broad Cultivation presents eight verses from 'diligence attains non-action' to 'cultivating the seven factors, no attachment in three realms.' Broad cultivation means: diligence motivated by joy (not fear), anchored in the Buddha's teaching, not retreating through heedlessness. The broad and great person is fortified by learning, walled by the Dharma, and defended by indomitable diligence. One-way travel, no retreat, swift liberation.
