第二十三章 · 安宁品

法句经 · Dhammapada

← 第二十二章 目录 Contents 第二十四章 →

健夫度苦岸，积有明行者，能以智慧行，是谓为安宁。心得安宁者，不为欲所动，不染于世法，此则无所著。积行者得乐，亦非无积者，精进无退减，是谓为安宁。不毁不恼人，如戒之所教，食知止足，独处自思惟。以慧自娱乐，行道为室家，非财为室家，亦非以车骑。当学此乐住，知足无所贪，离诸世俗缘，修行安乐住。有福者得乐，修道皆安宁，一切出苦恼，是谓安宁乐。胜己及他者，自证诸苦际，离一切恶法，此谓得安宁。信法戒具足，惭愧有所畏，以慧自净明，是谓得安宁。自强不放逸，能制胜情欲，制意成胜者，真安宁快乐。夫以沙门道，能度诸欲界，心已无所著，当尽于苦际。胜难胜难敌，胜己乃大胜，欲胜须自胜，后可胜于人。

逐句解释

健夫度苦岸，积有明行者，能以智慧行，是谓为安宁。

健壮的人度脱苦的彼岸，积累有明行者，能以智慧行事，这称为安宁。

The strong one crosses the shore of suffering; one who accumulates bright conduct; one who acts through wisdom — this is called peace.

Peace is not passive; it is won by the strong practitioner who accumulates wisdom-in-action and crosses the ocean of suffering.

心得安宁者，不为欲所动，不染于世法，此则无所著。

心得安宁者，不被欲望所动，不被世间法所染，此人则无所执著。

One whose mind has attained peace is not moved by desires; not stained by worldly dharmas — such a person has no attachment.

The mind at peace: unmoved by desire, unstained by the world. Not isolation but a different relationship to experience — nothing clings.

积行者得乐，亦非无积者，精进无退减，是谓为安宁。

积累行道者得乐，而不是没有积累的人，精进而无退减，这称为安宁。

Those who accumulate practice attain happiness; not those who have accumulated nothing; diligent without regressing — this is called peace.

Peace requires accumulation: consistent practice, diligence without regression. The lazy or inconsistent practitioner does not find this.

不毁不恼人，如戒之所教，食知止足，独处自思惟。

不毁谤不恼害他人，如同戒律所教导，饮食知足而止，独处而自我思惟。

Not disparaging, not harassing others; as the precepts teach; knowing when to stop with food; dwelling alone and contemplating oneself.

The four conditions of peace: non-harm in speech and action, following precepts, moderation in eating, and solitary contemplation.

以慧自娱乐，行道为室家，非财为室家，亦非以车骑。

以智慧自我娱乐，以修行道为家室，不以财货为家室，也不以车马为家室。

Taking wisdom as one's delight; making the path one's home; not making wealth the home; not making carriages and horses the home.

True home and delight defined: not material wealth or prestige but wisdom and the path. Where these are found, one is always at home.

当学此乐住，知足无所贪，离诸世俗缘，修行安乐住。

应当学习这种安乐住，知足而无所贪，离诸世俗缘，修行而安乐住。

One should learn this peaceful dwelling: knowing contentment without craving; separating from worldly conditions; practicing and dwelling in peace.

The practical teaching: contentment + non-craving + disengagement from worldly entanglements = peaceful dwelling.

有福者得乐，修道皆安宁，一切出苦恼，是谓安宁乐。

有福者得乐，修道者皆安宁，一切出离苦恼，这称为安宁之乐。

The fortunate attain happiness; all who cultivate the path are at peace; emerging from all suffering — this is called the joy of peace.

Peace is not merely calm but a positive joy that emerges when one steps out of the cycle of suffering.

胜己及他者，自证诸苦际，离一切恶法，此谓得安宁。

胜过自己及他人者，自证诸苦的边际，离一切恶法，这称为得到安宁。

Surpassing oneself and others; self-verifying the shore of all sufferings; departing from all evil dharmas — this is called attaining peace.

Peace comes from surpassing one's former self, verifying liberation through direct experience, and completely departing from harmful actions.

信法戒具足，惭愧有所畏，以慧自净明，是谓得安宁。

信法戒律具足，有惭愧有所畏，以智慧自我清净明亮，这称为得到安宁。

Faith in the Dharma and precepts fully accomplished; having shame and conscience; with wisdom self-purifying and brightening — this is called attaining peace.

Five components of peace: faith, precepts, shame, conscience, and wisdom. Together they produce purification.

自强不放逸，能制胜情欲，制意成胜者，真安宁快乐。

自强而不放逸，能制胜情欲，制御意念而成为胜者，真正安宁快乐。

Self-strengthening and not heedless; able to overcome emotions and desires; governing the mind and becoming victorious — truly peaceful and joyful.

The path to peace runs through self-strengthening and mind-governance. The one who masters their own mind has truly conquered.

夫以沙门道，能度诸欲界，心已无所著，当尽于苦际。

以沙门之道，能度脱诸欲界，心已无所执著，当彻底终尽苦的边际.

Through the path of the monk, one can cross the realms of desire; with a mind that has no attachment — one reaches the complete end of the shore of suffering.

The monk's path as the vehicle for crossing the desire realm. No attachment means no anchor; without anchor, the shore of suffering is reached.

胜难胜难敌，胜己乃大胜，欲胜须自胜，后可胜于人。

胜过难胜之敌，胜己才是大胜，欲胜须先自胜，后可胜于人。

Conquering difficult-to-conquer enemies — conquering oneself is the great conquest; to conquer, one must first conquer oneself; afterward one can conquer others.

The paradox of conquest: the greatest enemy is oneself. Only after genuine self-mastery can one genuinely aid others.

总结 · Summary

安宁品十二颂，定义真正的和平：非被动休息，而是主动修行的果实。不为欲动，不染世法；积行精进，知足离缘；信戒惭愧慧，五者俱足；制意成胜者，真安宁快乐。胜己乃大胜——安宁品以此结语，将和平与自我征服画上等号。

Peace presents twelve verses defining genuine peace as the fruit of active practice, not passive rest. Unmoved by desire, unstained by the world; accumulating practice with diligence and no regression; contentment, disengagement from worldly conditions; faith, precepts, shame, conscience, and wisdom — five components of purification. Governing the mind is the true conquest. The chapter closes: conquering oneself is the great conquest — peace and self-mastery are the same.
