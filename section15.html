第十五章 · 罗汉品

法句经 · Dhammapada

← 第十四章 目录 Contents 第十六章 →

去离忧患，脱于一切，缚结已解，冷而无暖。心净得念，无所贪乐，已度痴渊，如雁弃池。量腹而食，无所藏积，心空无想，度众行地。如空中鸟，远逝无碍，世间习尽，不复仰食。虚心无患，已到脱处，譬如飞鸟，暂下辄逝。制根从止，如马调御，舍憍慢习，为天所敬。不怒如地，不动如山，真人无垢，生死世绝。心已休息，言行亦正，从正解脱，寂然归灭。弃欲无着，缺三界障，望意已绝，是谓上人。在聚若野，平地高岸，应真所过，莫不蒙祐。

逐句解释

去离忧患，脱于一切，缚结已解，冷而无暖。

远去离开忧患，脱离于一切，束缚和结缚已经解开，清凉而无热恼。

Gone from sorrow and affliction; freed from everything; bonds and fetters already released; cool, with no burning heat.

The arahant's condition: liberation described as coolness (the literal meaning of 'nibbana'). The heat of passion, hatred, and delusion has been extinguished.

心净得念，无所贪乐，已度痴渊，如雁弃池。

心净而得正念，没有贪着和乐著，已经度越愚痴的深渊，如同大雁离弃池塘。

The mind is pure and mindful; without craving or delight; having crossed the abyss of delusion — like a goose leaving a pond.

The goose leaving the pond: it does not cling to the water or the food there. The arahant leaves the world's pleasures with the same ease and without looking back.

量腹而食，无所藏积，心空无想，度众行地。

量腹而食，不储藏积聚，心空无想，度过众生所行之地。

Eating only what the belly requires; nothing stored or accumulated; the mind empty, without thoughts; crossing the ground that ordinary beings traverse.

The arahant's material simplicity: eating only what is needed, accumulating nothing. The empty mind is not blank — it is free from the compulsive thinking that characterizes ordinary consciousness.

如空中鸟，远逝无碍，世间习尽，不复仰食。

如同空中的鸟，飞翔而无阻碍，世间习气已尽，不再仰望饮食（不再依赖世间）。

Like a bird in the sky, flying far without obstruction; worldly habits exhausted; no longer looking upward for sustenance.

Freedom of movement in the sky: the arahant is unobstructed by any worldly hold. The 'looking upward for food' suggests dependence on others or on circumstances — the arahant has moved beyond this.

虚心无患，已到脱处，譬如飞鸟，暂下辄逝。

心意虚空而无忧患，已到达解脱之处，就像飞鸟，短暂落下便立刻离去。

Empty mind, free from harm; already reached the place of liberation; like a flying bird that alights briefly and then leaves.

The bird that alights and leaves: the arahant may still be present in the world but is not held by it. Every landing is temporary; the sky is home.

制根从止，如马调御，舍憍慢习，为天所敬。

制约根门而从顺止息，如同调御好的马，舍弃骄慢习气，为天神所敬重。

Restraining the faculties and settling into stillness; like a well-trained horse; leaving behind pride and conceit — honoured by the gods.

The trained horse: powerful but controlled, moving only as directed. The arahant's controlled faculties are a similar combination of natural energy perfectly guided.

不怒如地，不动如山，真人无垢，生死世绝。

不嗔怒如大地，不动摇如高山，真人（阿罗汉）无垢染，生死世间的束缚已断绝。

Without anger like the earth; without moving like a mountain; the true person without defilement — the world of birth and death is severed.

Earth and mountain as images of the arahant's stability: the earth receives everything without resentment; the mountain stands through every storm. Both images point to a quality of being rather than doing.

心已休息，言行亦正，从正解脱，寂然归灭。

心已经休息，言语和行为也端正，从正而解脱，寂然归于涅槃。

The mind has rested; speech and conduct are also correct; liberated through the right; serenely returning to cessation.

The sequence of liberation: rest of mind → correct speech and conduct → liberation through the right → serene cessation. The 'rest' is not exhaustion but completion — the mind has arrived.

弃欲无着，缺三界障，望意已绝，是谓上人。

舍弃欲望而无执著，突破三界的障碍，望意已经断绝，这才叫做上等之人。

Abandoning desire without attachment; breaking through the obstructions of the three realms; aspirations severed — this is called a person of the highest kind.

The highest kind of person: not defined by birth, status, or accomplishment but by freedom — from desire, from the three realms, from even the aspiration that once drove the practice.

在聚若野，平地高岸，应真所过，莫不蒙祐。

无论在聚落还是旷野，无论平地还是高岸，真人（阿罗汉）所经过之处，无不蒙受庇护。

Whether in a village or the wilderness; on flat ground or on high banks; wherever the true person passes, all receive blessing.

The blessing that radiates from the arahant's presence: not through deliberate effort but through the quality of their being. The true person's passage through any environment is itself a gift.

总结 · Summary

罗汉品十颂，以一系列生动意象描绘阿罗汉（应真）的境界：清凉无热恼，如雁弃池，如鸟翔空，如地不嗔，如山不动。阿罗汉已断生死，心已休息，言行正当，所到之处令众生蒙祐。其境界不是强迫得来的，而是习气尽后的自然状态。

The chapter on the Arahant presents ten verses portraying the fully liberated person through a series of memorable images — cool as Nirvana, free as a bird in the sky, steady as a mountain, patient as the earth. The arahant has crossed the abyss of delusion, exhausted worldly habits, abandoned pride, and severed birth and death. Their presence in any environment — village or wilderness — is itself a source of blessing.
