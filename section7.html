为仁不杀，常能摄身，是处不死，所适无患。不杀为仁，慎言守心，是处不死，所适无患。彼乱已整，守以慈仁，见怒能忍，是为梵行。至诚安徐，口无粗言，不嗔彼所，是谓梵行。垂拱无为，不害众生，无所娆恼，是应梵行。常以慈哀，净如佛教，知足知止，是度生死。少欲好学，不惑于利，仁而不犯，世上所称。仁寿无犯，不兴变快，人为诤扰，慧以嘿安。普忧贤友，哀加众生，常行慈心，所适者安。仁儒不邪，安止无忧，上天卫之，智者乐慈。昼夜念慈，心无克伐，不害众生，是行无仇。不慈则杀，违戒言妄，过不与他，不观众生。酒致失志，为放逸行，后堕恶道，无诚不真。履仁行慈，博爱济众，有十一誉，福常随身。卧安觉安，不见恶梦，天护人爱，不毒不兵。水火不丧，在所得利，死升梵天，是为十一。若念慈心，无量不废，生死渐薄，得利度世。仁无乱志，慈最可行，愍伤众生，此福无量。假令尽寿命，勤事天下人，象马以祠天，不如行一慈。

逐句解释

为仁不杀，常能摄身，是处不死，所适无患。

行仁慈而不杀生，能够常常摄护身行，这样的人到达不死之境，走到哪里都无祸患。

Being kind and not killing; always able to restrain the body — such a one reaches the deathless and goes wherever without harm.

Non-killing as the first and most visible expression of loving-kindness. The protection it offers is not magical but logical — one who causes no harm accumulates no enemies.

不杀为仁，慎言守心，是处不死，所适无患。

不杀生便是仁，谨慎言语、守护内心，这样的人到达不死之境，走到哪里都无祸患。

Not killing is kindness; guarding speech and protecting the mind — such a one reaches the deathless and goes wherever without harm.

The same verse with speech and mind added: the three doors of karma — body, speech, and mind — must all be protected for loving-kindness to be complete.

彼乱已整，守以慈仁，见怒能忍，是为梵行。

他人的纷乱能够使其安定，以慈仁守护，见到嗔怒能够忍耐，这就是梵行。

Bringing order to what was disordered; guarding with kindness; seeing anger and bearing it — this is the holy life.

Loving-kindness is active: it doesn't merely avoid harm but moves to create peace where there was conflict, and absorbs anger without returning it.

至诚安徐，口无粗言，不嗔彼所，是谓梵行。

至诚而安详从容，口中没有粗鲁之言，不嗔恚他人的所作所为，这才叫梵行。

Sincere, calm and unhurried; no coarse words from the mouth; not angered by what others do — this is called the holy life.

The holy life is visible in manner as much as action: sincerity, unhurried ease, gentle speech, and non-reactivity define its texture.

垂拱无为，不害众生，无所娆恼，是应梵行。

顺其自然无所作为，不伤害众生，没有扰恼，这才符合梵行。

Effortless and unconditioned; not harming any living being; causing no disturbance — this accords with the holy life.

Wu-wei (无为) — effortless action — is here aligned with non-harm. The life of loving-kindness is not forced but flows naturally, disturbing no one.

常以慈哀，净如佛教，知足知止，是度生死。

常以慈悲心悲悯，清净如佛陀的教导，知足而知止，这是度越生死的方法。

Always acting with compassionate love; pure as the Buddha's teaching; knowing sufficiency and knowing when to stop — this crosses over birth and death.

Compassion, purity, contentment, and restraint together form the life that transcends the cycle. None of these qualities can be purchased; all must be cultivated.

少欲好学，不惑于利，仁而不犯，世上所称。

少欲而好学，不被利益迷惑，仁慈而不违犯，是世上所称赞的人。

Few desires and love of learning; not confused by profit; kind and not transgressing — this is who the world praises.

The qualities that earn genuine respect: simplicity, intellectual curiosity, non-greed, and the kindness that does not cross lines. These are recognized across cultures as marks of character.

仁寿无犯，不兴变快，人为诤扰，慧以嘿安。

仁者长寿而不违犯，不引起骚动和纷争，他人为争论所扰，仁者以沉默智慧而安住。

The kind person lives long without transgression; not stirring up change or conflict; while others are disturbed by argument, the wise stays secure in silence.

The silent response to conflict: not suppression but the stability of one whose mind is not touched by the provocation. Silence from strength is a form of loving-kindness.

普忧贤友，哀加众生，常行慈心，所适者安。

广泛关怀贤良的朋友，以悲悯心及于众生，常常修行慈心，所到之处带来安乐。

Widely caring for wise friends; extending compassion to all beings; always practising loving-kindness — wherever one goes, peace follows.

Universal loving-kindness is described as portable: it is not location-dependent. The practitioner of metta (loving-kindness) carries a field of peace that spreads to wherever they arrive.

仁儒不邪，安止无忧，上天卫之，智者乐慈。

仁者儒雅而不邪，安住而无忧，上天守护之，有智慧的人以慈为乐。

The kind person, refined and upright, rests in peace without worry; heaven protects them; the wise delight in loving-kindness.

The person of genuine loving-kindness is protected — not by magical intervention but by the quality of their relationships and the absence of enmity.

昼夜念慈，心无克伐，不害众生，是行无仇。

昼夜念慈，心中没有竞争与伐异，不伤害众生，这样的行持使人无冤无仇。

Mindful of loving-kindness day and night; the mind free of contention and conflict; not harming any being — such conduct makes one free of enemies.

Continuous loving-kindness meditation — day and night — as the practice that most directly dissolves enmity. No enemies arise because no hostile seeds have been planted.

不慈则杀，违戒言妄，过不与他，不观众生。

不慈则杀生，违犯戒律妄语，过失归咎于他人，不观察众生的痛苦。

Without kindness there is killing; the precepts are violated and speech is false; faults are blamed on others; one does not look at the suffering of beings.

The absence of loving-kindness is not neutral — it generates a cluster of negative behaviors: harm, dishonesty, blame, and blindness to others' pain.

酒致失志，为放逸行，后堕恶道，无诚不真。

饮酒导致失去智志，成为放逸之行，死后堕入恶道，无诚实不真实。

Intoxicants cause loss of wisdom; they lead to heedless conduct; afterward one falls to an evil realm; there is no sincerity or truth.

Intoxication is placed in the context of loving-kindness because it is the opposite of the clear, compassionate mind: it produces exactly the heedlessness and dishonesty that loving-kindness cannot coexist with.

履仁行慈，博爱济众，有十一誉，福常随身。

实践仁道、修行慈心、博爱帮助众生，有十一种称誉，福德常随其身。

Walking the path of kindness, practising loving-kindness, loving broadly and helping all beings — there are eleven forms of praise; merit follows such a one always.

The eleven praises of loving-kindness practice are detailed in the following verses — a comprehensive list of the visible fruits of metta.

卧安觉安，不见恶梦，天护人爱，不毒不兵。

睡时安稳，醒时安稳，不见恶梦，天神守护，人所爱戴，不受毒害，不受兵刃。

Sleeping peacefully; waking peacefully; free from evil dreams; protected by heavenly beings; loved by people; unharmed by poison or weapons.

Six of the eleven fruits of loving-kindness: the quality of rest, the absence of nightmares, divine protection, human affection, and immunity from violence and poison.

水火不丧，在所得利，死升梵天，是为十一。

不受水火之灾，在所到之处都能获得利益，死后升上梵天，这便是十一种福德。

Not harmed by water or fire; gaining benefit wherever one goes; at death ascending to the Brahma realm — these are the eleven.

The remaining fruits: protection from elemental disasters, universal good fortune, and favourable rebirth. The point is not literal magic but the accumulated consequence of a life spent in loving-kindness.

若念慈心，无量不废，生死渐薄，得利度世。

若修念慈心，无量而不废弃，生死轮回渐渐减薄，得利而度越世间。

If one cultivates loving-kindness mindfulness without limit and does not abandon it, birth and death gradually thin; one gains benefit and crosses the world.

The cumulative effect of sustained loving-kindness practice: gradual reduction of the conditions that fuel rebirth, leading eventually to liberation.

仁无乱志，慈最可行，愍伤众生，此福无量。

仁者心志不乱，慈是最可修行的，悲悯众生，此福德无量。

The kind person's resolve is undisturbed; loving-kindness is the most practicable path; having compassion for beings — this merit is immeasurable.

The chapter's summary claim: loving-kindness is not an advanced practice for the few — it is the most practicable of all paths, available to everyone, and generating immeasurable merit.

假令尽寿命，勤事天下人，象马以祠天，不如行一慈。

即使终其一生，勤勤恳恳地侍奉天下人，甚至以象马祭祀诸天，也比不上修行一念慈心。

Even if one spent an entire lifetime diligently serving all people in the world, and sacrificed elephants and horses to the gods — it is not equal to cultivating a single moment of loving-kindness.

The climactic verse: no amount of external service or ritual offering matches the inner act of loving-kindness. The teaching is radical — interiority matters more than any external gesture.

总结 · Summary

慈仁品十九颂，系统阐述慈悲不杀的功德与修行。真正的慈仁是行于梵行之中的：不仅不伤害，更能安抚纷乱，忍受嗔怒，以安静之态回应争论。品末列举修慈的十一种福德，并以最高赞叹收结：终其一生服侍众人或祭祀诸天，也不如行一念慈心。慈心是最可修行的法门，其福德无量。

The chapter on Loving-kindness presents nineteen verses on the practice and fruits of metta — universal compassion and non-harm. The holy life is defined through gentle speech, patience with anger, and effortless non-interference. The chapter enumerates eleven fruits of loving-kindness practice — from peaceful sleep to heavenly rebirth — and closes with the radical claim that a single moment of genuine loving-kindness surpasses all external service and ritual offering.
