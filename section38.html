道利品第三十八，道以利益众生，利益以道为本。若能以大乘，度人于苦海，是则为道利，利人为最善。人能建大道，度苦到彼岸，若施以财物，不如施以法。一失道之利，万劫难可复，道之利益者，后世得安乐。以施得财宝，以法得解脱，财宝可失尽，解脱永不失。利以正法化，不以刀杖来，能令众生喜，此乃为大利。

逐句解释

道利品第三十八，道以利益众生，利益以道为本。

道利品第三十八，以道利益众生，利益以道为本。

The chapter on the Way and Benefit — the way benefits all beings; benefit is rooted in the way.

Opening statement of the chapter's theme: way and benefit are inseparable. The path produces benefit; benefit is what the path produces.

若能以大乘，度人于苦海，是则为道利，利人为最善。

若能以大乘，度人于苦海，这则为道利，利人为最善。

If one can with the great vehicle, ferry people across the sea of suffering — this is the way-and-benefit; benefiting others is the highest good.

The great vehicle (mahāyāna perspective enters): ferrying others across the sea of suffering is the highest expression of way-and-benefit.

人能建大道，度苦到彼岸，若施以财物，不如施以法。

人能建大道，度苦到彼岸，若施以财物，不如施以法。

A person who can establish the great path, crossing suffering to the other shore — if giving material wealth, it does not equal giving the Dharma.

Dharma-gift surpasses material gift: both are generosity, but the Dharma-gift liberates permanently while material gifts address temporary needs.

一失道之利，万劫难可复，道之利益者，后世得安乐。

一旦失去道之利，万劫难以复得，道之利益者，后世得安乐。

Once the benefit of the path is lost, ten thousand kalpas are difficult to recover; one who benefits from the path — in future lives attains peace and happiness.

The enormous stakes: losing the path-benefit requires vast time to recover. The path-benefit accumulated produces peace across future lives.

以施得财宝，以法得解脱，财宝可失尽，解脱永不失。

以布施得财宝，以法得解脱，财宝可以失尽，解脱永不失去。

Through generosity one attains wealth; through the Dharma one attains liberation; wealth can be completely lost; liberation is never lost.

The permanent vs. the impermanent: wealth (however obtained by good means) can be lost; liberation, once genuinely attained, is permanent.

利以正法化，不以刀杖来，能令众生喜，此乃为大利。

以正法利化，不以刀杖来，能令众生喜悦，这才是大利。

Benefiting through the correct Dharma; not through rods and weapons; able to cause beings to rejoice — this is the great benefit.

The great benefit is not coerced: it comes through the Dharma, not through force, and its mark is that beings rejoice.

道利品终。

道利品终。道以利益众生，法施胜财施，解脱永不失，以正法化令众生喜——此是最大道利。

End of the chapter on the Way and Benefit. The way benefits beings; Dharma-gift surpasses material gift; liberation is never lost; benefiting through the correct Dharma, causing beings to rejoice — this is the greatest way-and-benefit.

Closing summary.

总结 · Summary

道利品六颂，以道利为主题：道利益众生，法施胜财施，解脱永不失。以大乘度人于苦海是最善；失道之利万劫难复；以正法化令众生喜是大利。道与利不可分——真正的利益以道为根。

The Way and Benefit presents six verses on the inseparability of path and benefit. Ferrying others across the sea of suffering through the great vehicle is the highest good. Dharma-gift surpasses material gift: the Dharma liberates permanently. Once the path-benefit is lost, vast time is needed to recover it. Liberation, once attained, is never lost — unlike material wealth. The great benefit comes not through force but through the Dharma, and its mark is that beings rejoice.
