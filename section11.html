第十一章 · 心意品

法句经 · Dhammapada

← 第十章 目录 Contents 第十二章 →

意使作狗，难护难禁，慧正其本，其明乃大。轻躁难持，唯欲是从，制意为善，自调则宁。意微难见，随欲而行，慧常自护，能守即安。独行远逝，覆藏无形，损意近道，魔系乃解。心无住息，亦不知法，迷于世事，无有正智。念无适止，不绝无边，福能遏恶，觉者为贤。佛说心法，虽微非真，当觉逸意，莫随放心。见法最安，所愿得成，慧护微意，断苦因缘。有身不久，皆当归土，形坏神去，寄住何贪？心豫造处，往来无端，念多邪僻，自为招恶。是意自造，非父母为，可勉向正，为福勿回。藏六如龟，防意如城，慧与魔战，胜则无患。

逐句解释

意使作狗，难护难禁，慧正其本，其明乃大。

心意像猎犬一样，难以守护和禁制，以智慧端正其根本，其光明才能广大。

The mind acts like a hound — hard to guard, hard to restrain; wisdom corrects its root and then its brilliance is great.

The hound metaphor for mind: untrained, it ranges widely after every scent of sensation. Wisdom is not a leash but the training that redirects the hound's energy toward what matters.

轻躁难持，唯欲是从，制意为善，自调则宁。

心意轻浮躁动难以持守，只随欲望而走，制约内心是善，自我调伏则宁静。

Restless and hard to hold, following only desire — restraining the mind is good; self-taming brings peace.

The restless mind is its own prison: always running, never resting. The counter-movement — restraint and self-taming — produces the peace the running mind can never find.

意微难见，随欲而行，慧常自护，能守即安。

心意微细难见，随欲望而行，以智慧常自护，能够守住则安稳。

The mind is subtle and hard to see; it follows desire; wisdom constantly self-guards; one who can maintain it is at peace.

Subtlety is the challenge: desire enters through channels too fine to observe casually. Continuous mindfulness is needed precisely because the mind is hard to see.

独行远逝，覆藏无形，损意近道，魔系乃解。

心意独行而远逝，覆藏无形，损减心意则接近道，魔的束缚才能解开。

The mind goes alone and far, concealed and formless — diminishing the mind brings one close to the path; Mara's bonds are thereby loosened.

The formless mind travels without a body — it can be anywhere, immediately. The instruction to 'diminish' the mind means to reduce its self-amplifying tendencies through sustained mindfulness.

心无住息，亦不知法，迷于世事，无有正智。

心无处安住、不得停息，也不了知正法，迷于世间事务，没有正智。

The mind has no resting place and does not know the Dharma; deluded by worldly affairs, without right wisdom.

The untrained mind's condition: constant movement, ignorance of what matters, and absorption in distractions. This is not failure — it is simply the default state that practice addresses.

念无适止，不绝无边，福能遏恶，觉者为贤。

念头没有停歇，绵绵不绝无有边际，福德能够遏制恶念，觉悟者才是贤者。

Thoughts have no stopping point, endless and boundless; merit can check evil; the one who awakens is truly wise.

The boundless stream of thought is not a problem to be solved but a condition to be understood. Merit — accumulated good action — gradually shapes this stream, and awakening redirects it entirely.

佛说心法，虽微非真，当觉逸意，莫随放心。

佛说心法，虽然微妙但不是（执著的）真实，应当觉察散逸的心意，不要随着放逸的心走。

The Buddha says the mind-dharma, though subtle, is not real in the sense of something to cling to; one should awaken to a wandering mind and not follow a heedless mind.

Even the teaching about mind is not to be grasped as a fixed object. The instruction is practical: notice when the mind wanders, and do not follow it down its habitual paths.

见法最安，所愿得成，慧护微意，断苦因缘。

见到正法最为安稳，所愿皆能成就，以智慧守护微细的心意，断除苦的因缘。

Seeing the Dharma is the greatest peace; wishes are fulfilled; wisdom guards the subtle mind and cuts the causes of suffering.

Seeing the Dharma — direct insight into the nature of experience — is described as the greatest peace because it addresses suffering at its root rather than managing its symptoms.

有身不久，皆当归土，形坏神去，寄住何贪？

有此身体不会长久，皆当归还于土地，形体坏灭神识离去，寄住于此身何必贪著？

This body will not last; all must return to earth; when the form breaks down, the spirit departs — dwelling here temporarily, what is there to crave?

The 'guest' metaphor: we are temporary occupants of the body, not its owners. Craving it makes as little sense as a hotel guest fighting to keep the room forever.

心豫造处，往来无端，念多邪僻，自为招恶。

心意提前营造去处，来来往往无有端绪，念头多邪僻，自招恶报。

The mind creates its destinations in advance; it comes and goes without end; when thoughts are mostly wrong and crooked, one invites evil upon oneself.

Pre-destination: the mind imagines its future experiences and thereby creates the conditions for them. Crooked thinking generates crooked outcomes — not magically but through the choices it motivates.

是意自造，非父母为，可勉向正，为福勿回。

这是内心自己所造，不是父母所为，可以努力向正，为福而不回头。

This is what the mind itself creates — not what parents make; one can strive toward the right and toward merit without turning back.

Personal responsibility without blame: the mind creates its own condition, but this is liberating rather than condemning — it means the mind can also uncreate those conditions.

藏六如龟，防意如城，慧与魔战，胜则无患。

收藏六根如龟缩壳，防护心意如守城池，以智慧与魔战斗，战胜则无忧患。

Concealing the six senses like a tortoise pulling in its limbs; guarding the mind like defending a city; wisdom battles Mara — victory means freedom from harm.

Two images for sense restraint: the tortoise (complete, effortless withdrawal) and the city (active, strategic defense). Both point to the same quality of inner protection.

总结 · Summary

心意品十二颂，深入观察心意的本质：微细难见，如犬难驯，轻躁难持，随欲而行，往来无端。但心意也是一切的造作者，善恶皆由自造，非父母为。因此，以智慧端正心意根本，护意如守城，与魔战而胜，是修行的核心工程。

The chapter on the Mind presents twelve verses on the nature and challenge of the mind — subtle, restless, following desire, creating its own conditions. Yet precisely because the mind is self-created, it can also be self-corrected. Wisdom is the tool: guarding the subtle mind like a city's defenses, battling Mara's forces, and ultimately cutting the causes of suffering at their root.
