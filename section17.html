第十七章 · 恶行品

法句经 · Dhammapada

← 第十六章 目录 Contents 第十八章 →

见善不从，反随恶心，求福不正，反乐邪淫。凡人为恶，不能自觉，愚痴快意，令后郁毒。凶人行虐，沈渐数数，快欲为人，罪报自然。吉人行德，相随积增，甘心为之，福应自然。妖淫见福，其恶未熟，至其恶熟，自受罪虐；贞祥见祸，其善未熟，至其善熟，必受其福。击人得击，行怨得怨，骂人得骂，施怒得怒。世人无闻，不知正法，生此寿少，何宜为恶？莫轻小恶，以为无殃，水渧虽微，渐盈大器，凡罪充满，从小积成。莫轻小善，以为无福，水滴虽微，渐盈大器，凡福充满，从纤纤积。夫士为行，好之与恶，各自为身，终不败亡。好取之士，自以为可，没取彼者，人亦没之。恶不即时，如构牛乳，罪在阴祠，如灰覆火。戏笑为恶，以作身行，号泣受报，随行罪至。作恶不覆，如兵所截，牵往乃知，已堕恶行，后受苦报，如前所习。如毒摩疮，船入洄澓，恶行流衍，靡不伤克。加恶诬罔人，清白犹不污，愚殃反自及，如尘逆风坌。过失犯非恶，能追悔为善，是明照世间，如日无云曀。夫士所以行，然后身自见，为善则得善，为恶则得恶。有识堕胞胎，恶者入地狱，行善上升天，无为得泥洹。非空非海中，非隐山石间，莫能于此处，避免宿恶殃。众生有苦恼，不得免老死，唯有仁智者，不念人非恶。

逐句解释

见善不从，反随恶心，求福不正，反乐邪淫。

见到善法不去追随，反而随从恶心；求福不走正道，反而乐于邪淫。

Seeing good but not following it; instead following an evil mind; seeking merit but not rightly; instead delighting in wrong conduct.

The perverse inversion: seeing good but choosing evil, seeking merit through wrong means. This is not ignorance but willful misdirection.

凡人为恶，不能自觉，愚痴快意，令后郁毒。

凡夫作恶，不能自我觉察，以愚痴为快意，导致日后苦毒。

Ordinary people doing evil cannot notice themselves doing it; finding pleasure in delusion, they create future poison.

Unconsciousness of evil while it is being done: the ordinary person's condition is not deliberate malice but inability to see their own actions clearly as they perform them.

凶人行虐，沈渐数数，快欲为人，罪报自然。

凶恶之人行虐，渐渐沉沦越来越多，以快欲为常，罪报自然而至。

The cruel person acts viciously, sinking gradually and repeatedly; making pleasure their conduct — the consequence of sin naturally comes.

The gradual deepening of vice: each harmful act makes the next one easier. The consequence is not imposed but simply the natural continuation of the trajectory.

吉人行德，相随积增，甘心为之，福应自然。

吉祥之人修行功德，随行积增，甘心为之，福报自然应来。

The auspicious person cultivates virtue, accumulating and increasing step by step; doing so willingly — merit naturally responds.

The mirror image: virtue also deepens with each act, and the person who chooses it willingly accelerates the accumulation. Merit flows toward what it was sown in.

妖淫见福，其恶未熟，至其恶熟，自受罪虐。贞祥见祸，其善未熟，至其善熟，必受其福。

邪淫者见到福，是因为恶尚未成熟，待到恶熟，自受罪的虐待；贞正吉祥者见到祸，是因为善尚未成熟，待到善熟，必受其福。

The corrupt seem to prosper because their evil has not yet ripened; when it does ripen, they suffer their own sin. The virtuous seem to suffer because their good has not yet ripened; when it does, they receive their blessing.

Timing of karma: the apparent prosperity of the wicked and suffering of the good is a matter of timing — both will ripen. The teaching requires a longer view than daily observation.

击人得击，行怨得怨，骂人得骂，施怒得怒。

打人得打，以怨待人得怨，骂人得骂，施怒于人得怒。

Strike someone and you get struck; act with enmity and you get enmity; abuse someone and you get abuse; send anger and you get anger.

The mirror principle of karma: what you send out returns. Not as cosmic punishment but as the natural response of a world in which every action generates its echo.

世人无闻，不知正法，生此寿少，何宜为恶？

世间众生无有闻思，不知道正法，活在这短暂的寿命中，何宜作恶？

People in the world without hearing, not knowing the right Dharma — born into this brief life, why do evil?

The rhetorical question addresses the combination of ignorance and short life: given how little time there is and how little most people understand, making it even shorter through evil conduct makes no sense.

莫轻小恶，以为无殃，水渧虽微，渐盈大器，凡罪充满，从小积成。

不要轻视小恶，以为没有灾殃，水滴虽然微小，渐渐盈满大器，凡种种罪的充满，是从小积累而成。

Do not dismiss small evils as causing no harm; water drops though tiny gradually fill a large vessel — all sin filling up is accumulated from the small.

The most vivid accumulation image in the Dhammapada: the dripping water that eventually fills the barrel. Small wrongs dismissed as inconsequential are the very ones that fill the vessel of consequence.

莫轻小善，以为无福，水滴虽微，渐盈大器，凡福充满，从纤纤积。

不要轻视小善，以为没有福报，水滴虽然微小，渐渐盈满大器，凡种种福的充满，是从点滴积累而成。

Do not dismiss small goods as causing no merit; water drops though tiny gradually fill a large vessel — all merit filling up is accumulated from the smallest.

The same image applied to virtue: small acts of kindness, however unremarkable, accumulate into the fullness of merit. The teaching is an argument for not dismissing the daily ordinary.

夫士为行，好之与恶，各自为身，终不败亡。

人的所作所为，无论好与恶，各自由自身承担，终究不会消灭。

A person's conduct — whether good or evil — each acts for that person's own body; in the end it does not perish.

Personal accountability: one's actions are exactly and only for oneself — no one else bears them. And karma does not perish; it waits to ripen.

好取之士，自以为可，没取彼者，人亦没之。

喜欢强取的人，自以为可以，强取别人的，人们也会强取他的。

One who loves to take by force thinks it acceptable; those who take from others — others will take from them.

The social mirror: the person who normalizes taking by force creates a world in which that norm applies to them as well.

恶不即时，如构牛乳，罪在阴祠，如灰覆火。

恶不会即时应报，就像刚挤的牛奶，罪在隐处供养，就像灰覆盖火焰。

Evil does not immediately appear, like fresh milk; sin dwells in the dark, like embers covered with ash.

The hidden nature of consequence: fresh milk looks innocent; ash looks cold. Both contain what is not yet visible. Karma works like these — quietly, until conditions for ripening are met.

戏笑为恶，以作身行，号泣受报，随行罪至。

戏笑着作恶，以此为身体的行为，哭泣号泣地受报，随行为而罪至。

Laughing while doing evil, making it the body's practice — weeping to receive the consequence; sin follows the action as it comes.

The tonal contrast: lightness while doing evil, heaviness when receiving its consequence. The laughter is not punished — it is simply incompatible with what is coming.

作恶不覆，如兵所截，牵往乃知，已堕恶行，后受苦报，如前所习。

作恶不加遮覆，如同被兵器截断，被牵引而去才知道，已经堕入恶行，之后受苦报，如同之前的习气。

Doing evil without concealment, like being cut by a weapon; dragged away, one then knows one has fallen into evil conduct — afterward receiving the painful consequence according to previous habituation.

The moment of recognition: dragged toward the consequence, one finally knows. The habituation that produced the action is also what shapes the consequence.

如毒摩疮，船入洄澓，恶行流衍，靡不伤克。

就像毒液摩擦伤口，船陷入旋涡，恶行流泛蔓延，无不受到伤害。

Like poison rubbed into a wound; like a boat entering a whirlpool — evil conduct spreads and flows, harming without exception.

Two images of the spreading of evil: poison in a wound deepens every moment, and a boat in a whirlpool cannot extract itself by rowing harder. Evil, once established, has its own momentum.

加恶诬罔人，清白犹不污，愚殃反自及，如尘逆风坌。

以恶言诬陷清白之人，清白者仍然不受污染，愚者的灾殃反而落在自己身上，就像尘土逆风扑面。

Falsely accusing with evil words — the pure remain unpolluted; the fool's calamity rebounds upon themselves, like dust blown back against the wind.

The boomerang of false accusation: it cannot stick to the genuinely innocent, and returns to harm the one who threw it. Like dust against the wind.

过失犯非恶，能追悔为善，是明照世间，如日无云曀。

过失违犯而有恶行，能够追悔改正向善，这是照耀世间的光明，如同日出而无云障。

Having made a fault and committed wrong conduct; able to pursue repentance and turn to good — this is the light that illuminates the world, like the sun without cloud.

Repentance as sunlight: the turn from wrong toward right, when genuine, illuminates both the person and their surroundings. The cloudless sun is the image of a cleared conscience.

夫士所以行，然后身自见，为善则得善，为恶则得恶。

人的所作所为，事后自身会见到，为善则得善，为恶则得恶。

A person's conduct — afterward they see it themselves; do good and you get good; do evil and you get evil.

Direct seeing: eventually one's conduct becomes visible to oneself. The cause-effect relationship is simple and direct, requiring no middleman.

有识堕胞胎，恶者入地狱，行善上升天，无为得泥洹。

有意识的众生堕于胞胎，恶者入于地狱，行善者上升天界，无为者得涅槃。

Beings with consciousness fall to the womb; the evil enter hell; the good ascend to heaven; the unconditioned attain Nirvana.

The four destinations according to conduct: rebirth, hell, heaven, and Nirvana. Only the last represents genuine liberation from the cycle.

非空非海中，非隐山石间，莫能于此处，避免宿恶殃。

不论在空中还是海中，不论隐于山石之间，没有任何地方能够避免宿世恶业的灾殃。

Not in the sky, not in the ocean, not hidden in mountain caves — there is nowhere one can avoid the calamity of past evil.

No escape from karma: the geography is total (sky, ocean, caves), and the conclusion is absolute. There is no outside from which to watch karma operate on someone else.

众生有苦恼，不得免老死，唯有仁智者，不念人非恶。

众生有苦恼，不能免除老死，唯有仁慈有智慧的人，不记念他人的过失与恶行。

Beings have affliction and cannot escape aging and death; only the kind and wise do not dwell on others' faults and evils.

The final verse: the universal affliction of aging and death is acknowledged, and the one distinguishing quality of those who have genuinely progressed — they do not keep score of others' wrongs.

总结 · Summary

恶行品二十二颂，详细阐述恶行的运作机制：小恶积成大罪（如水渧满器），恶不即时显现（如灰覆火），恶报无处可逃（非空非海中）。但品章不止于警示，也给出了希望：能追悔向善，照世如日出；清白之人不受污；仁智者不念人非恶。

The chapter on Evil Deeds presents twenty-two verses on the mechanics of harmful action and its consequences. Small evils dismissed as harmless fill up a vessel drop by drop; evil covered by ash is still fire; karma cannot be escaped by any geography. But the chapter also contains its own hope: repentance genuinely pursued illuminates like the sun, false accusation rebounds on the accuser, and the kind and wise do not keep account of others' wrongs.
