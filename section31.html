第三十一章 · 象喻品

法句经 · Dhammapada

← 第三十章 目录 Contents 第三十二章 →

我如象斗战，以弓所加箭，忍受于骂辱，世多好恶言。譬象调正行，可中王所乘，忍辱不恚谦，是则最善乘。若得调正象，可往于战场，骑从王者乘，一切之所叹。自调为善乘，调而后乘马，调御为象乘，善哉自调御。人不自调者，不学他调者，若不自调御，焉能调彼人？象虽大身形，多力所伤甚，忍辱不恚者，军中之大将。生而不死者，所有之寿命，应当求脱离，不见苦恼已。当安徐如象，进退以知时，如象知进退，大象可调御。

逐句解释

我如象斗战，以弓所加箭，忍受于骂辱，世多好恶言。

我如战场上的象，以弓箭所射加，忍受骂辱，世间多好（说）恶言。

I am like an elephant in battle struck by arrows from a bow; enduring slander and abuse — the world is full of those who love harsh words.

The elephant in battle as the image of patient endurance. The practitioner, like the war elephant, must absorb the arrows of criticism and abuse without breaking.

譬象调正行，可中王所乘，忍辱不恚谦，是则最善乘。

譬如象调正而行，可作为王者所乘，忍辱不嗔恚而谦逊，这是最善的乘骑。

Like an elephant well-trained in conduct, fit to be ridden by a king — patient, without anger, and humble: this is the most excellent mount.

The royal elephant as the model of the trained mind: disciplined, fit for the highest service, combining patience, non-anger, and humility.

若得调正象，可往于战场，骑从王者乘，一切之所叹。

若得调正之象，可往于战场，骑随王者乘，一切人所赞叹。

If one obtains a well-trained elephant, one can go to the battlefield; riding alongside the king — praised by all.

The metaphor extended: the trained practitioner, like the trained elephant, can go into the most difficult situations (the battlefield of life) and be praised.

自调为善乘，调而后乘马，调御为象乘，善哉自调御。

自我调御为善乘，调御之后可乘马，调御为象乘，善哉，自我调御！

Self-governance is the excellent mount; after governing oneself one can ride the horse; self-governance is the elephant-ride; excellent — self-governance!

The escalating metaphor: self-governance as the mount, the horse, and finally the royal elephant. The practitioner who governs themselves is on the highest vehicle.

人不自调者，不学他调者，若不自调御，焉能调彼人？

人若不自我调御，不学习被他人调御，若不自我调御，怎能调御他人？

One who does not govern themselves; who does not learn from being governed by others; if one does not govern oneself — how can one govern others?

Self-governance as prerequisite: without governing oneself, governing others is impossible. This must come first.

象虽大身形，多力所伤甚，忍辱不恚者，军中之大将。

象虽形体巨大，多力（攻击）所伤甚深，忍辱不恚者，（如同）军中大将。

The elephant, though its body is huge, is greatly wounded by the many forces; one with patience and without anger — is a great general in the army.

The great general's quality is not power but patience. The elephant is huge and strong yet easily wounded; the patient one without anger is the true commander.

生而不死者，所有之寿命，应当求脱离，不见苦恼已。

生而不死者，所有的寿命，应当求脱离，不再见苦恼。

For one born who does not die — however long the lifespan — one should seek liberation, no longer seeing suffering and vexation.

While still alive, seek liberation. The opportunity of life is precisely this: to use the lifespan to escape the cycle of suffering-and-return.

当安徐如象，进退以知时，如象知进退，大象可调御。

应当安稳从容如象，进退以知时宜，如象知进退，大象可调御。

One should be as steady and unhurried as an elephant; in advancing and retreating, knowing the right time; as an elephant knows advance and retreat — the great elephant can be trained.

Timing as the elephant's wisdom: knowing when to advance and when to retreat. The trainable elephant is also the teachable practitioner.

象喻品终。

象喻品终。自调御为最善乘，忍辱不恚为军中大将，安徐知时如象——象喻品以大象之德，喻修行之要。

End of the chapter on the Elephant. Self-governance is the most excellent mount; patience without anger is the great general; steady, unhurried, knowing the right time — the elephant-chapter uses the elephant's virtues to illustrate what practice requires.

Closing summary.

总结 · Summary

象喻品八颂，以战象受箭喻修行者受辱，以调御之象喻自我调御。调正之象可乘于王，自我调御者为最善乘。忍辱不恚者为军中大将——大象的优势不在力气而在耐心。安徐知时，进退有度，大象可御——修行者亦然。

The Elephant presents eight verses using the elephant as the image of patient, trained practice. Like a war-elephant absorbing arrows, the practitioner endures slander. The well-trained elephant is the king's vehicle; self-governance is the most excellent mount. Patience without anger is the quality of the great general — the elephant's greatness lies not in strength but in endurance. Steady, unhurried, knowing when to advance and retreat — the trainable elephant is also the teachable practitioner.
