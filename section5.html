第五章 · 戒慎品

法句经 · Dhammapada

← 第四章 目录 Contents 第六章 →

人而常清，奉律至终，净修善行，如是戒成。慧人护戒，福致三宝，名闻得利，后上天乐。常见法处，护戒为明，得成真见，辈中吉祥。持戒者安，令身无恼，夜卧恬淡，寤则常欢。修戒布施，作福为福，从是适彼，常到安处。何终为善？何善安止？何为人宝？何盗不取？戒终老安，戒善安止，慧为人宝，福盗不取。比丘立戒，守摄诸根，食知自节，悟意令应。以戒降心，守意正定，内学正观，无忘正智。明哲守戒，内思正智，行道如应，自清除苦。蠲除诸垢，尽慢勿生，终身求法，勿暂离圣。戒定慧解，是当善惟，都已离垢，无祸除有。着解则度，余不复生，越诸魔界，如日清明。狂惑自恣，已常外避，戒定慧行，求满勿离。持戒清净，心不自恣，正智已解，不睹邪部。是往吉处，为无上道，亦舍非道，离诸魔界。

逐句解释

人而常清，奉律至终，净修善行，如是戒成。

一个人始终保持清净，终身奉持戒律，净化善行，如此戒律才算真正成就。

A person who is always pure, who upholds the precepts to the end, who purifies good conduct — in this way virtue is fully accomplished.

Virtue is not a single act but a continuous orientation — a life maintained in clarity from beginning to end.

慧人护戒，福致三宝，名闻得利，后上天乐。

有智慧的人护持戒律，福德归向三宝，名声流传并获得利益，死后升上天界享乐。

The wise person guards the precepts; merit flows toward the Three Jewels; reputation and benefit follow; afterward one ascends to heavenly joy.

Virtue is not merely ascetic restraint — it actively generates merit, reputation, and spiritual benefit across multiple dimensions of existence.

常见法处，护戒为明，得成真见，辈中吉祥。

常常见到正法之所在，护持戒律为智慧之光，得成真正的见解，在同辈中最为吉祥。

Always seeing the place of the Dharma, protecting the precepts as one's light, attaining right view — such a one is the most auspicious among their peers.

The correlation of precepts with insight: virtue is not only a preparation for wisdom — it is itself a form of clarity, guarding the conditions that allow right view to emerge.

持戒者安，令身无恼，夜卧恬淡，寤则常欢。

持守戒律的人身心安稳，令身体无恼，夜间安卧恬静，清醒时常常欢喜。

One who keeps the precepts is at peace; the body is free from affliction; at night one rests calmly; upon waking one is always glad.

The immediate experiential fruit of virtue: physical ease, undisturbed sleep, and waking gladness. Virtue is not self-denial but the removal of the inner friction that causes constant low-grade suffering.

修戒布施，作福为福，从是适彼，常到安处。

修持戒律并布施，行善以积福，由此走向彼岸，常到安乐之处。

Cultivating virtue and giving, creating merit through merit — by this one moves toward the other shore and always arrives at a place of peace.

The pairing of virtue (internal) and generosity (relational): together they create momentum toward liberation. Virtue restrains the self; generosity opens it.

何终为善？何善安止？何为人宝？何盗不取？

究竟以何为善？以何善行安住？以何为人生的宝贝？有什么财富是盗贼无法夺取的？

What is the final good? What good gives lasting rest? What is the human treasure? What is the wealth that no thief can take?

The four rhetorical questions set up the answer in the next verse. They are the questions of someone who has seen through the inadequacy of ordinary answers.

戒终老安，戒善安止，慧为人宝，福盗不取。

戒律至老都带来安稳，戒律使善安住，智慧是人生的宝贝，而福德是盗贼无法夺取的。

Virtue gives peace into old age; virtue establishes the good; wisdom is the human treasure; merit is what no thief can take.

The four answers are striking in their economy. Virtue and wisdom do not decline with age or circumstance; merit accumulated through good action is genuinely inalienable.

比丘立戒，守摄诸根，食知自节，悟意令应。

比丘确立戒律，守护摄持诸根，饮食知道自我节制，觉悟内心使其相应于法。

The monk establishes virtue, guards and restrains the faculties, knows moderation in eating, and trains the mind to accord with the Dharma.

Four practices in sequence: establish ethics, guard the senses, moderate consumption, train the mind. This is the foundational daily structure of monastic practice.

以戒降心，守意正定，内学正观，无忘正智。

以戒律调伏内心，守护内心进入正定，内在修习正观，不忘失正智。

Through virtue the mind is subdued; guarding the mind enters right stillness; internally cultivating right insight — right wisdom is never forgotten.

Virtue is not just behavior management — it tames the mind itself, creating the inner stability needed for meditative collectedness and insight.

明哲守戒，内思正智，行道如应，自清除苦。

明智者守持戒律，内心思惟正智，行道如法相应，自然清净除苦。

The wise guard the precepts and inwardly contemplate right wisdom; practising the path as it should be practised, one purifies oneself and removes suffering.

This verse captures the unity of the threefold training: virtue (precepts), wisdom (right wisdom), and meditation (practising the path) work together as a single system of purification.

蠲除诸垢，尽慢勿生，终身求法，勿暂离圣。

除去种种垢染，彻底去除我慢，终身求法，片刻不要离开圣者。

Removing every defilement; exhausting pride and not letting it arise; seeking the Dharma one's whole life; never departing from the noble ones — even for a moment.

The four commitments of a lifelong practice: clean up the mind, flatten the ego, pursue the teaching continuously, and stay close to those who embody it.

戒定慧解，是当善惟，都已离垢，无祸除有。

戒、定、慧、解脱，这四者应当善加思惟，完全离垢，无祸患地除去有漏（烦恼）。

Virtue, stillness, wisdom, liberation — these should be well contemplated; having completely left defilement, one removes the taints without disaster.

The four stages of the Buddhist path: virtue, concentration, wisdom, liberation — presented not as a linear sequence but as a unified field of practice to be contemplated together.

着解则度，余不复生，越诸魔界，如日清明。

执取解脱则能度越，余下的不再生起，越过种种魔界，如同太阳清净明朗。

Grasping liberation, one crosses over; nothing remains to be reborn; transcending all the realms of Mara — clear and brilliant as the sun.

The image of the sun transcending all shadow is perfect for the liberated mind: not just free of this or that obstacle, but radiantly clear in all directions.

狂惑自恣，已常外避，戒定慧行，求满勿离。

狂乱迷惑和放逸自恣，已经常常向外回避，戒定慧的修行，应当求其圆满而不离弃。

Madness, delusion, and self-indulgence — these are to be continually avoided; the practice of virtue, stillness, and wisdom should be pursued to completion without abandonment.

Three things to avoid and three to pursue — the negative and positive are stated together to make the choice explicit. Completeness (满) is the goal; abandonment is the danger.

持戒清净，心不自恣，正智已解，不睹邪部。

持戒清净，心不放纵，正智已经解脱，不再见到邪见之部众。

With pure virtue and a mind not given to indulgence, having attained right wisdom and liberation — one no longer encounters the realm of wrong views.

Virtue and non-indulgence create a mind that has simply outgrown wrong views — not by fighting them but by inhabiting a space they cannot reach.

是往吉处，为无上道，亦舍非道，离诸魔界。

这是通往吉祥之处的道路，是无上的道，也舍弃了非道，远离了一切魔界。

This is the path to the auspicious place, the unsurpassed path; it also abandons non-paths and leaves behind all realms of Mara.

The final verse of the chapter captures the dual movement of the path: going toward liberation while simultaneously leaving behind everything that is not liberation.

总结 · Summary

戒慎品十六颂，系统阐述戒律的深义与功效。戒律不仅是行为规范，更是调伏内心、进入正定的基础，是通向智慧与解脱的门径。持戒者身安、心净、夜眠恬淡、日间欢喜；戒与慧结合，是人生最高的财富，也是盗贼无法夺取的内在庇护。

The chapter on Precepts presents sixteen verses on the nature and power of ethical discipline. The precepts are not merely behavioral rules but the foundation of mental composure, right view, and liberation. Their fruits are immediate — physical ease, undisturbed sleep, daily gladness — and ultimate — crossing over to the deathless shore. Virtue is the inalienable treasure, the unsurpassable path.
