第三十三章 · 利养品

法句经 · Dhammapada

← 第三十二章 目录 Contents 第三十四章 →

如果贪利养，失道以不轨，贪利养非道，正言示正路。慧者不贪利，不以利惑意，求利非法者，迷失于正道。贪利若不修，后当受苦报，利养以苦出，修道以苦除。苦哉利养者，苦哉比丘行，非利以妨道，道不受利养。利养堕冥渊，烦恼以自缠，此为利养患，非道之所行。犯戒求利养，是为非道行，求利非法者，是谓邪道行。求利而放逸，坏精进本业，是以不得利，身后亦受苦。

逐句解释

如果贪利养，失道以不轨，贪利养非道，正言示正路。

如果贪求利养，就会失道而不循正轨，贪利养不是道，正言指示正路。

If one is greedy for gain and support, one loses the path and goes off the track; craving gain and support is not the path; right speech points to the right road.

The chapter's thesis: craving for material support (利养) is incompatible with the path. Right speech and right road are offered as the corrective.

慧者不贪利，不以利惑意，求利非法者，迷失于正道。

智慧者不贪利，不以利养惑乱意念，求利以非法者，迷失于正道。

The wise do not crave gain; they do not let gain confuse the mind; those who seek gain through wrong means are lost from the right path.

The wise person's relationship with gain: not craving it, not being confused by it. Wrong-means seeking leads directly to losing the path.

贪利若不修，后当受苦报，利养以苦出，修道以苦除。

贪利而不修行，后当受苦果报，利养从苦而出，修道以除苦。

Craving gain without cultivating practice — afterward one must receive the fruit of suffering; gain and support come from suffering; practice eliminates suffering.

The cycle: craving gain without practice → suffering fruit. Gain-and-support themselves arise from suffering. Practice is what breaks the cycle.

苦哉利养者，苦哉比丘行，非利以妨道，道不受利养。

苦哉，贪利者；苦哉，（以贪利）行的比丘；非利（之求）以妨碍道，道不受利养（所动）。

Suffering indeed for those who crave gain; suffering indeed for monks who act for gain; non-gain (in its seeking) obstructs the path; the path is not moved by gain and support.

The lament: suffering for the gain-craver and suffering for the monk who acts for gain. The path itself is unmoved by gain — the practitioner must share that quality.

利养堕冥渊，烦恼以自缠，此为利养患，非道之所行。

利养使人堕入黑暗深渊，烦恼以此自我缠绕，这是利养之患，非道所行之处。

Gain and support cause one to fall into the dark abyss; defilements entangle one thereby; these are the dangers of gain and support — not what the path practices.

The abyss of gain: material support becomes the mechanism of spiritual entanglement. Defilements use it to bind the practitioner.

犯戒求利养，是为非道行，求利非法者，是谓邪道行。

犯戒而求利养，这是非道行，求利以非法者，这称为邪道行。

Violating precepts to seek gain and support — this is non-Dharmic conduct; seeking gain through wrong means — this is called wrong-path conduct.

Two-part warning: precept-violation for gain is non-Dharmic; wrong-means gain-seeking is the wrong path. The two are related but distinct.

求利而放逸，坏精进本业，是以不得利，身后亦受苦。

求利而放逸，破坏精进的本业，因此不得利，身后也受苦。

Seeking gain with heedlessness; destroying the root-work of diligence — thereby not attaining gain; after death also receiving suffering.

Paradox of gain-seeking: heedless pursuit of gain destroys the diligence that would actually produce wellbeing, leading to both present loss and future suffering.

利养品终。

利养品终。贪利失道，慧者不惑于利，利养以苦出，修道以苦除。道不受利养所动——此是修行者的自由。

End of the chapter on Gain and Support. Craving gain loses the path; the wise are not confused by gain; gain comes from suffering, practice eliminates suffering. The path is not moved by gain — this is the practitioner's freedom.

Closing summary.

总结 · Summary

利养品七颂，专论物质供养之危险。贪利失道，慧者不惑；利养以苦出，修道以苦除。利养堕冥渊，烦恼以自缠。道不受利养所动——修行者以此为自由。求利而放逸，反坏精进，得不偿失，身后受苦。

Gain and Support presents seven verses exclusively on the spiritual danger of material support. Craving gain loses the path; the wise are not confused by it. Gain-and-support itself arises from suffering; practice eliminates suffering. Material support causes one to fall into the dark abyss of entanglement. The path is unmoved by gain — the practitioner must share that quality. Heedless gain-seeking destroys the very diligence that would produce genuine wellbeing.
