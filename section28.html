第二十八章 · 道行品

法句经 · Dhammapada

← 第二十七章 目录 Contents 第二十九章 →

八直最上道，四谛为正说，不淫为上行，施灯为明尊。此道无有边，彼无忧畏境，以此界度彼，人当自净心。吾已告汝道，拔去众苦刺，当以自精进，知道自行道。汝等当自修，如来不能为，已入禅定者，得脱魔罗缚。一切行无常，以慧观照者，即得厌苦患，是道得清净。一切行是苦，以慧观照者，即得厌苦患，是道得清净。一切法无我，以慧观照者，即得厌苦患，是道得清净。应起而不起，应去而不去，贪惰自奔驰，是则为损减。所行非善道，自以为善道，人趣放逸路，魔罗之所诱。若人不能知，此道不能行，以是行余道，无益自煎熬。道为知者铺，路为行者履，从此世彼世，自利利群生。

逐句解释

八直最上道，四谛为正说，不淫为上行，施灯为明尊。

八正道是最上之道，四谛是正确的说法，不淫是最上行，施灯为明者之尊。

The Eightfold Path is the most excellent path; the Four Truths are the correct teaching; non-lust is the most excellent conduct; giving light is the venerable of the luminous.

The Buddha's core teaching in miniature: the Eightfold Path, Four Truths, and two specific virtues (non-lust, generosity of light/knowledge).

此道无有边，彼无忧畏境，以此界度彼，人当自净心。

此道无有边际，彼（涅槃）是无忧畏的境界，以此（道）度越彼（苦界），人应当自净其心。

This path has no limit; that (Nirvana) is the realm without worry or fear; crossing to that with this (path) — a person should purify their own mind.

The unlimited path leading to the limitless realm. The requirement: self-purification of the mind. No one else can do it.

吾已告汝道，拔去众苦刺，当以自精进，知道自行道。

我已告诉你此道，拔去众多苦刺，应当以自我精进，知道而自行此道。

I have already shown you the path — it removes all thorns of suffering; you should with your own diligence, knowing the path, walk it yourself.

The Buddha's final statement to each practitioner: I have shown it; now you must walk it. No vicarious practice.

汝等当自修，如来不能为，已入禅定者，得脱魔罗缚。

你等应当自我修行，如来不能（为你）做，已进入禅定者，得脱魔罗之缚。

You must practice yourselves; the Tathāgata cannot do it for you; those who have entered meditation — are freed from the bonds of Mara.

The most explicit statement of the Buddha's role: teacher, not savior. Meditation entered, Mara's bonds are broken.

一切行无常，以慧观照者，即得厌苦患，是道得清净。

一切行（为）无常，以智慧观照者，即得厌离苦患，这是道的清净。

All formations are impermanent; one who observes with wisdom — immediately attains weariness of suffering; this is the purity of the path.

Impermanence + wisdom-contemplation = weariness of suffering = path-purity. The seeing of impermanence with wisdom naturally produces the turning away from suffering.

一切行是苦，以慧观照者，即得厌苦患，是道得清净。

一切行（为）苦，以智慧观照者，即得厌离苦患，这是道的清净。

All formations are suffering; one who observes with wisdom — immediately attains weariness of suffering; this is the purity of the path.

The second characteristic applied: suffering seen clearly with wisdom produces the same result — weariness and purification.

一切法无我，以慧观照者，即得厌苦患，是道得清净。

一切法无我，以智慧观照者，即得厌离苦患，这是道的清净。

All dharmas are without self; one who observes with wisdom — immediately attains weariness of suffering; this is the purity of the path.

The three characteristics as a triple path: impermanence, suffering, non-self — each observed with wisdom produces liberation.

应起而不起，应去而不去，贪惰自奔驰，是则为损减。

应当起时而不起，应当去时而不去，贪惰自行奔驰，这是减损。

Not rising when one should rise; not going when one should go; craving and laziness run of themselves — this is diminishment.

The cost of sloth: missed opportunities, craving and laziness taking over, and the gradual diminishment of the practitioner.

所行非善道，自以为善道，人趣放逸路，魔罗之所诱。

所行非善道，自以为是善道，人趣向放逸之路，是魔罗所诱惑的。

What is practiced is not the good path, yet one thinks it is the good path; a person heading toward the path of heedlessness — is being led by Mara.

Mara's subtlest work: not open temptation but the quiet substitution of heedlessness for genuine practice, while convincing the practitioner they are on the right path.

若人不能知，此道不能行，以是行余道，无益自煎熬。

若人不能了知此道，不能行此道，以此（无知）行其他道，无益而自煎熬。

If a person cannot know this path, cannot walk this path — walking other paths through this (ignorance) is without benefit and causes self-torment.

Wrong paths are not merely ineffective but actively harmful: they cause the practitioner to 'self-torment' — expending effort that produces no liberation.

道为知者铺，路为行者履，从此世彼世，自利利群生。

道为知者所铺，路为行者所履，从此世到彼世，自利而利群生。

The path is laid out for those who know; the road is walked by those who practice; from this world to the next — benefiting oneself and all beings.

The path requires both knowledge (understanding) and practice (walking). Knowledge without walking, or walking without understanding, is incomplete. Both together produce benefit across lives.

勤行道，仁者以佛语。

勤行此道，仁者！以佛语为依，知无常苦无我，得苦际。

Practice the path diligently, benevolent one! Relying on the Buddha's words — knowing impermanence, suffering, and non-self, attaining the shore of suffering's end.

The direct exhortation to the practitioner: diligence, reliance on the teaching, the three characteristics, liberation. The chapter's essential message condensed.

总结 · Summary

道行品十二颂，是全书最具系统性的修道论。八正道最上，四谛正说；三相（无常、苦、无我）以慧观照各得清净；汝等当自修，如来不能为。道为知者铺，路为行者履——知与行缺一不可。魔罗之诱不在明处，在于以放逸冒充善道。知此道，行此道，自利利群生。

The Path presents twelve verses as the most systematic account of practice in the T0210. The Eightfold Path is most excellent; the Four Truths are the correct teaching; the three characteristics observed with wisdom each produce purity. Most importantly: you must practice yourselves — the Tathāgata cannot do it for you. The path is laid out for those who know; it is walked by those who practice. Mara's subtlest work is convincing practitioners they are on the right path while they drift into heedlessness.
