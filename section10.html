第十章 · 放逸品

法句经 · Dhammapada

← 第九章 目录 Contents 第十一章 →

戒为甘露道，放逸为死径，不贪则不死，失道为自丧。慧智守道胜，终不为放逸，不贪致欢喜，从是得道乐。常当惟念道，自强守正行，健者得度世，吉祥无有上。正念常兴起，行净恶易灭，自制以法寿，不犯善名增。发行不放逸，约以自调心，慧能作定明，不返冥渊中。愚人意难解，贪乱好诤讼，上智常重慎，护斯为宝尊。莫贪莫好诤，亦莫嗜欲乐，思心不放逸，可以获大安。放逸如自禁，能却之为贤，已升智慧阁，去危为即安。明智观于愚，譬如山与地，居乱而身正，彼为独觉悟。是力过师子，弃恶为大智，睡眠重若山，痴冥为所弊。安卧不计苦，此以常受胎，不为时自恣，能制漏得尽。自恣魔得便，如师子搏鹿，能不自恣者，是为戒比丘。彼思正净者，常当自护心，比丘谨慎乐，放逸多忧愆。变诤小致大，积恶入火焰，守戒福致善，犯戒有惧心。能断三界漏，此乃近泥洹，若前放逸，后能自禁，是照世间，念定其宜。过失为恶，追覆以善，是照世间，念善其宜。少庄舍家，盛修佛教，是照世间，如月云消。人前为恶，后止不犯，是照世间，如月云消。生不施恼，死而不戚，是见道悍，应中勿忧。断浊黑法，学惟清白，度渊不反，弃猗行止，不复染乐，欲断无忧。

逐句解释

戒为甘露道，放逸为死径，不贪则不死，失道为自丧。

戒律是甘露之道，放逸是死亡之路，不贪则不死，失去正道是自我毁灭。

The precepts are the path of nectar; heedlessness is the road of death; not craving, one does not die; losing the path is self-destruction.

The chapter opens with the starkest possible contrast: virtue as the path of life, heedlessness as the road of death. Not metaphorically — in the deepest sense.

慧智守道胜，终不为放逸，不贪致欢喜，从是得道乐。

以智慧守道最为殊胜，终究不为放逸，不贪则获得欢喜，由此得到道的乐趣。

Wisdom guarding the path — supreme, never heedless; freedom from craving brings joy; from this one gains the bliss of the path.

Wisdom as the guardian of practice: it watches over the tendency toward heedlessness and keeps the practitioner on course. The reward is not deferred — the bliss of the path begins now.

常当惟念道，自强守正行，健者得度世，吉祥无有上。

应当常常思惟正道，自强奋进守持正行，勇健者得以度越世间，吉祥无有比这更高的了。

Always contemplate the path; strengthen yourself and guard right conduct; the vigorous cross over the world; there is no higher auspiciousness.

Contemplation, self-strengthening, right conduct, vigour — four qualities that together enable crossing over. The 'crossing' is Nibbana — the highest good named directly.

正念常兴起，行净恶易灭，自制以法寿，不犯善名增。

正念常常生起，净化行为则恶容易消灭，以正法自制而延长寿命，不违犯则善名增长。

Right mindfulness constantly arising; conduct purified and evil easily extinguished; self-restrained through the Dharma, one's life is extended; not transgressing, good reputation grows.

The practical fruits of diligence: purification of conduct, control of harmful impulses, extended life, and good reputation. These are verifiable, immediate benefits.

发行不放逸，约以自调心，慧能作定明，不返冥渊中。

发起修行而不放逸，以节制来调伏内心，智慧能作禅定的光明，不再返回黑暗的深渊。

Setting out in practice without heedlessness; using restraint to tame the mind; wisdom creates the light of stillness; one does not return to the dark abyss.

Practice as departure from the darkness: once genuine wisdom-light is kindled, return to the abyss of ignorance becomes increasingly difficult. Each step forward makes the next step easier.

愚人意难解，贪乱好诤讼，上智常重慎，护斯为宝尊。

愚人的心意难以教化，贪乱喜欢争讼，上智者常常慎重，把这种谨慎作为宝贵的守护。

The fool's mind is hard to instruct, attached to disorder and fond of quarrelling; the supremely wise are always careful and guarded — holding this caution as a precious treasure.

Caution as treasure: the wise person knows how easily the mind slides toward heedlessness, and so values the habit of carefulness the way a merchant values their most precious goods.

莫贪莫好诤，亦莫嗜欲乐，思心不放逸，可以获大安。

不要贪心，不要喜欢争讼，也不要沉迷于欲乐，以心思惟而不放逸，可以获得大安乐。

Do not crave; do not love quarrelling; do not be addicted to sensual pleasure; with a mind that reflects and is not heedless, one can attain great peace.

Three things to avoid and one to cultivate: the three avoidances cover the main forms of distraction, and mindful non-heedlessness is the single positive practice that replaces all three.

放逸如自禁，能却之为贤，已升智慧阁，去危为即安。

放逸就像自我囚禁，能够却除放逸的人是贤者，已经登上智慧的楼阁，从危险走向即时的安乐。

Heedlessness is like self-imprisonment; one who can push it back is wise; having ascended the tower of wisdom, one goes from danger to immediate peace.

The self-imprisonment image: heedlessness is not freedom — it is the cage. The person who overcomes it ascends to a higher vantage point from which the danger is visible and can be avoided.

明智观于愚，譬如山与地，居乱而身正，彼为独觉悟。

明智者看待愚人，就如同高山俯视平地，居于纷乱之中而自身端正，那人是独自觉悟的人。

The wise look down upon the foolish as a mountain looks down upon the ground; dwelling among the disordered while remaining upright — such a person awakens alone.

The mountain and ground: not a judgment of worth but a description of perspective. From higher ground, the dangers of the lower are visible. The solitary awakening in the midst of disorder is a powerful image of individual responsibility for liberation.

是力过师子，弃恶为大智，睡眠重若山，痴冥为所弊。

这种力量超过狮子，舍弃恶法是大智慧；沉睡重如山，愚痴的黑暗使人受弊。

This strength surpasses that of a lion; abandoning evil is great wisdom. Heavy sleep is like a mountain; the darkness of delusion oppresses one.

Two contrasting images: the lion's strength as less than the power of genuine wisdom-based renunciation; and heavy sleep as the mountain of delusion that weighs down the unawakened.

安卧不计苦，此以常受胎，不为时自恣，能制漏得尽。

安卧而不计苦，因此常常受胎（再生），不在适当时机节制自己，就不能制止烦恼漏尽。

Lying at ease without reckoning the cost — such a one constantly returns to the womb; not restraining oneself at the right moment, one cannot master the taints to exhaustion.

Heedless comfort and repeated rebirth: those who take their ease without practice keep feeding the conditions for continued existence. Timely restraint is what breaks the cycle.

自恣魔得便，如师子搏鹿，能不自恣者，是为戒比丘。

放逸自恣，魔就得到机会，就像狮子扑鹿一样；能够不放逸自恣的人，才是持戒的比丘。

Where there is self-indulgence, Mara finds an opening — like a lion pouncing on a deer; one who is not self-indulgent — this is a monk who keeps the precepts.

The predator metaphor: Mara (the force of delusion and death) does not attack fortified positions but hunts the exposed and unguarded. The precepts are the protective wall.

彼思正净者，常当自护心，比丘谨慎乐，放逸多忧愆。

那些思惟正净者，应当常常自护内心，比丘以谨慎为乐，放逸则多忧患和过失。

Those who contemplate the pure and right should always protect the mind; a monk who is careful is joyful; one who is heedless has many worries and faults.

Guardedness as joy: the careful practitioner is not anxious but genuinely happy — the vigilance that protects them from harm is itself a source of wellbeing.

变诤小致大，积恶入火焰，守戒福致善，犯戒有惧心。

细小的争论变成大争，积累恶业如同投入火焰，守持戒律福德归向善，违犯戒律则心生恐惧。

Small quarrels grow to large ones; accumulated evil enters the flames; keeping the precepts draws good merit toward the good; breaking them brings a fearful mind.

Escalation: small wrongs grow. The flame image for accumulated karma: sufficient fuel, and something catches fire. Virtue's dynamic is the reverse — each act of virtue draws more good toward it.

能断三界漏，此乃近泥洹，若前放逸，后能自禁，是照世间，念定其宜。

能够断除三界的烦恼漏，这才是接近涅槃，若之前放逸，后来能够自我禁止，这照耀世间，以正念定夺其所宜。

One who can cut the taints of the three realms is close to Nirvana; if formerly heedless but later able to restrain oneself — this illuminates the world; mindfulness determines what is appropriate.

The turning point: even previous heedlessness does not disqualify one from liberation. The capacity to turn back from heedlessness is itself illuminating — it shows that change is always possible.

过失为恶，追覆以善，是照世间，念善其宜。少庄舍家，盛修佛教，是照世间，如月云消。

曾经有过失行恶，追悔并以善覆盖，这照耀世间，应当忆念善法；年少强壮时舍家，盛力修习佛教，这照耀世间，如同月亮从云中出来。

Past fault and evil, pursued and covered by good — this illuminates the world; mindfulness of good determines what is right. In youth and vigour, leaving home and training fully in the Buddha's teaching — this illuminates the world, like the moon emerging from cloud.

Three forms of illumination: repentance covered by good action; mindfulness of the good; and the brightness of youthful, vigorous practice. The moon from clouds is one of the most enduring images of awakening.

人前为恶，后止不犯，是照世间，如月云消。生不施恼，死而不戚，是见道悍，应中勿忧。

人若前期行恶，后来止住不再违犯，这照耀世间，如月从云中出来。生时不施加苦恼，死时不感悲戚，这是见道的勇健，应于其中无所忧虑。

If formerly doing evil but then stopping and not transgressing — this illuminates the world like the moon from cloud. Not inflicting suffering in life, not grieving in death — this is the vigour of one who has seen the path; in the midst of it all, no worry.

Transformation as illumination: the person who genuinely turns from harm is like the moon breaking through — not despite the darkness but because of it. And the one who has seen the path lives without imposing suffering and dies without grief — the fruits are tangible.

断浊黑法，学惟清白，度渊不反，弃猗行止，不复染乐，欲断无忧。

断除浊黑之法，修学清净白净之法，度越深渊不再返回，放弃依倚而行止，不再被乐染着，欲断则无忧。

Cutting the turbid, dark practices; training only in what is pure and clear; crossing the abyss without returning; abandoning all dependence; no longer stained by pleasure; when desire is cut, there is no worry.

The complete arc of diligence: from cutting darkness through to the complete liberation from craving. The absence of worry when desire is cut is described as natural consequence, not acquired skill.

总结 · Summary

放逸品二十颂，以戒为甘露道、放逸为死径开篇，强调不放逸的核心地位。真正的勤奋不仅是努力，更是持续的正念与自制：不贪、不诤、不沉迷欲乐，以智慧守护正道。即使曾经放逸，只要能够回头，便能如月从云出，照耀世间。品末以'欲断无忧'作结，揭示了不放逸的最终果实：完全的自由。

The chapter on Heedlessness opens with the starkest contrast in the Dhammapada: virtue is the path of nectar; heedlessness is the road of death. Twenty verses explore diligence as the most fundamental spiritual quality — more powerful than a lion, more illuminating than sunlight, capable of transforming even a previously heedless life. The chapter closes with liberation as the natural fruit of sustained non-heedlessness: when desire is cut, there is simply no more worry.
